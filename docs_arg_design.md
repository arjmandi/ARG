# ARG — Aligned Referent Grounding
## v3 (Revised Design — round-2 must-change critiques integrated)

ARG is a new system, not a Sensi revision. No Sensi organ survives by name; only mechanisms validated with single-variable attribution survive, as constraints, with their evidence cited inline. The representation, the loop, and the claim are designed together so that one pre-registered factorial — the separate removal of one join and one agenda — can kill the whole thesis cleanly.

---

## 1. Thesis

**The unification claim — a measured decomposition, not an asserted identity.** The literature treats four problems as separate: symbol grounding (Floridi's circumvention critique), long-range cross-reference failure (BOOKCOREF/LegalCore-class), salience and positional bias (lost-in-the-middle, context-rot), and goal drift in agents (HIPIF's "objective drift"). ARG claims these share one failure surface — and it decomposes the agent-side symptom into two mechanically separable components so the claim can be measured rather than defined into truth:

> **Goal drift decomposes into a binding component and an abandonment component; the binding component is claimed to be referent-grounding failure under salience bias.**
> — **GDS-bind** (within-context binding loss): the intention and its referents are both present in context, but the join between them lives only in transformer attention, which loses bindings under distance and dullness — the FitM/LONGPIBENCH regime. Repaired by **J**, the goal–referent join (§2.5): a persistent structure that canonicalizes referents from consequence-tested evidence, binds every active goal to its referents, and mechanically re-surfaces both, pre-joined, at fixed prompt positions every turn.
> — **GDS-abandon** (cross-call commitment loss): the intention is simply absent from later contexts, and drift compounds through local errors and sparse feedback — the HIPIF regime — even when every context is short and the goal maximally salient. Repaired by **A**, the external Commitment store executed by deterministic code (§5.3), which is a state-persistence mechanism, not a positional one.
> The unification result is a pre-registered **double dissociation**: killing J degrades the grounding metrics and GDS-bind together while GDS-abandon stays flat; killing A degrades GDS-abandon and ICR while the grounding metrics stay flat — plus two cross-surface transfer predictions (X1, X2 below) that test whether the two repairs help each other's metric family. Both drift components are defined in **every** factorial cell — including agenda-off cells — via the §8 shadow-agenda instrumentation, so neither half of the dissociation rests on an undefined metric.

The evidence is assigned to its correct component. The binding component's existence proof is the fact-xref single variable: identical stack, one mechanical coordinate join added, 0/200 → 3/3 L1 (G2 vs G3) — prose "plate at (20,31)" and render "C17 color0 3c at (21,31)" never joined across ~2,300 actions / 13 runs until the join was mechanical. The abandonment component's existence proof is the mid-tier run that wrote the winning move verbatim (t150, t184), wrote its own unstick prescription (t60), and executed none across 171 consecutive un-committed turns: the intention was present, recent, and salient — position and salience were not the failure; the missing external commitment store was. Frontier models at high effort sometimes hold both in attention (one L1, once); mid-tier models never reliably do, and prompt rhetoric restores neither. ARG externalizes both, separately, and measures them separately.

**Falsifiable form.** Pre-registered predictions, evaluated under the full protocol (≥3 unseen ARC-AGI-3 games × ≥3 seeds, mean ± stdev, backbone swaps, full call/token accounting, and the §8 compute-parity rule — every headline comparison prints its parity row, and any FULL advantage carried at ρ > 1 must survive the compute-matched comparator):

- **P0 (efficacy floor):** mid-tier + full ARG completes levels on ≥3 unseen games. *A ~0-wins result is a structural defeater of the entire contribution, not a caveat; no efficiency or learning claim survives it.*
- **P1 (load-bearing structure):** the join-off cell (J0, §8 factorial) completes zero or significantly fewer levels than full ARG. *Falsified if* J0 matches full ARG, **or** if the §8 compute-matched J0 variant closes the gap.
- **P2 (unification — the headline claim, as a double dissociation):** across the §8 2×2 factorial {J on/off} × {A on/off}: killing J moves RGR, DTL, and **GDS-bind** jointly while GDS-abandon stays flat; killing A moves **GDS-abandon** and ICR while RGR/DTL stay flat. *Falsified if* GDS-bind fails to track J and position, or the effects cross over, or the §8 compute-matched cell variants close the claimed deltas.
- **X1 / X2 (cross-surface transfer):** **X1** — the join-only cell (A off) shows higher goal-adherence (GA) than the neither cell: a pure grounding intervention reduces drift. **X2** — the agenda-only cell (J off) shows better RGR/DTL than the neither cell: a pure persistence intervention improves grounding, via commitments forcing ConsequenceRecords on dull goal-bound referents. *Each carries its own falsified-if (null result).*
- **P2b (prior-art delta):** the **B-CACHE** baseline (§8) — a LINK-KG-equivalent alias cache — reproduces LINK-KG-class de-fragmentation on canonicalization metrics (mention-resolution rate, duplicate-referent rate) but leaves completions, GA, and ICR at bare-backbone levels; the full-ARG-minus-B-CACHE delta on task-conditioned metrics is the claimed contribution. *Falsified if* B-CACHE matches full ARG on completions — then the contribution reduces to domain transfer of a prompt cache and the unification thesis is dead — or if the §8 compute-matched B-CACHE variant closes the delta.
- **P3 (transfer):** the same code path, zero game-specific tokens, moves a position-controlled long-context/goal benchmark (BABILong/LONGPIBENCH/PlanBench-verification class) exactly where bare backbones collapse — middle positions, spaced referents, late-horizon adherence. In this instantiation the grounding claim deflates to **deterministic referential anchoring within a formal corpus**; no world-grounding is claimed (§2.3). *Falsified if* gains appear only on ARC games.
- **P4 (tier claim):** ARG-full on a small open-weight backbone (Kaggle-eligible hardware) retains a stated fraction of Sonnet-backbone performance, with backbone-swap arms separating architecture effect from model effect, and with **WER reported per backbone** (§2.1) so "the small model cannot emit into the structure" is mechanically separable from "the structure does not transfer to small models." *Falsified if* the architecture effect vanishes under swap with WER within floor.

**Pre-committed downgrade clause.** If X1 and X2 are both null while full ARG beats all factorial cells, the headline automatically reframes from "one failure with four surfaces" to "a shared failure surface addressed by one artifact," and §8 must then include LINK-KG-style alias-cache and HIPIF-style progress-folding as named head-to-head baselines on the long-context and ARC tracks respectively. Either outcome is publishable; the claim's scope is an experimental output, not an authorial choice.

All efficiency and learning claims are **conditioned on task success**: win/completion rates are reported first; action counts and cost appear only for completed levels; a 0-completion cell prints "0 completions" and no efficiency number, ever. The retired 50–94× efficiency headline does not reappear in any form.

**What this is not.** Not curriculum-driven (the goal ladder plus agenda governs *doing*; curriculum is an optional, ablatable seed — §3.2). Not program induction: every prior result ran on typed prose facts plus geometric evidence while the verified-program tier held zero rows across 1,580+ transitions ever; ARG's load-bearing substrate is prose-with-typed-slots plus geometry, by design. Not frontier amortization: every environment is entered cold; nothing is pre-trained on games — simultaneously the owner's bar, the ARC-AGI-3 ethos, and what the hidden Phase-B evaluation set enforces. **Not a solution to the symbol grounding problem:** ARG claims causal-informational grounding relative to a formal micro-world only — the scoped claim of §2.3 — and positions itself as an implementation of Floridi's own sanctioned route: curation, verification, and tooling as the legitimate means of expanding what a system can do soundly, without attributing understanding. The contribution is exactly: *preserve and present, for medium and small models, the structure frontier models sometimes hold in attention.*

## 2. The ARG Structure

### 2.1 Design stance: the LLM proposes, the Executive disposes

The core tension — "a structure that is both keeping the structure AND LLMs can manipulate and consume well" — is resolved by one rule enforced at every boundary:

> **No LLM output ever mutates the store directly, and no LLM ever reads a raw dump.** Every write is a typed proposal in a closed vocabulary, schema-validated on ingest; every state transition (grounding rungs, goal status, promotion, demotion, eviction, fission, **agenda revision**) is executed by deterministic code — the **Executive** — against invariants the LLM cannot forge. Every read is a mechanically rendered view under a fixed serialization contract. The LLM manipulates *proposals* and consumes *views*; the structure is never in its hands.

Symmetrically, consumption is not optional: the view renderer is a materialized consumption path, so certified knowledge cannot sit inert (the standing counterexample: the exact win rule certified at score 8 while live state asserted "win condition UNKNOWN" — the judge gated truth but nothing gated *use*).

Every producer→consumer surface ships with a schema validator, a byte-level golden-file round-trip test, and an adapter-integrity canary (nonce echo; image-receipt probe on any vision path). A malformed response is a hard, logged failure, never a silent degrade — LLM-adapter retries have silently blinded perception before (the dspy JSONAdapter image-drop, twice), and 21 triage defects establish that legibility between organs is where designs die.

**Write-Path Integrity clause.** Rejection is bounded, defined at exhaustion, and measured:

- **Retry budget.** Every LLM write carries a validation budget of at most **2 typed re-prompts**. The second re-prompt automatically decomposes emission to **one op type per call** (LINK-KG's sequential-extraction move, invoked only on failure, so the modal beat's cost is unchanged). On exhaustion the proposal set is **dropped**, the beat proceeds, and the ChangeSet remains flagged *unexplained* — a standing B5 re-trigger and a T4 stall input — and a `WRITE_REJECT` row `{organ, op_type, violation_class, retry_count, turn_id}` is appended to Log. Exhaustion never stalls the beat and never silently degrades.
- **Completeness invariant.** The validator rejects any Observer response that fails to return a verdict (`R###` or `NEW`) for **every** Executive-proposed `component_hash`. Silence on a candidate is a violation, not an implicit skip — the Executive holds the candidate list and enforces coverage.
- **Measured write-path error rate.** **WER** (rejected emissions / total write calls, per organ, per backbone) and **DPR** (dropped-proposal rate after retry exhaustion), both computed from `WRITE_REJECT` rows, join the §8 metric table and the Q13 meta-metric floors. Rejections under the §5.3 Revision-Evidence gate meter into WER like any other ingest rejection. A WER floor breach prints a named **"write-path degradation"** diagnosis row and **disqualifies that run from architecture attribution** in P1/P2/P4 cells (exactly as Q11 does for plumbing defects). WER is reported per backbone in the P4 swap matrix, so a small backbone's inability to emit into the schema is a printed number, never a confound.

### 2.2 Schema (typed records)

Records are append-only where they carry evidence; status is always *computed*, never overwritten. **Last-writer-wins is forbidden everywhere** — keys are (run, id, version), so a failed run can never overwrite a successful run's knowledge (the carryover-notes anti-selection failure).

```
Referent {
  id: R###                    # stable, Executive-issued — never LLM-minted
  kind: enum {percept-cluster, region, action, signal}
                              # MACHINE-DERIVED ONLY: closed mapping from Locator
                              # provenance (GridRegion → percept-cluster;
                              # ActionSlot → action; adapter-declared signal
                              # channel → signal). No LLM ever writes kind.
  controllable: HYPOTHESIS | TESTED | DEMOTED | null
                              # computed, revisable annotation replacing the old
                              # agent-self kind value: earned like any Rule from
                              # ConsequenceRecords (movement-command ↔ correlated
                              # self-diff receipts), demotable, TTL on negatives
  anchor: Locator             # MACHINE-WRITTEN ONLY (see below)
  signature: hash             # normalized shape × color multiset — re-identification across frames
  label: str?                 # LLM-proposed, cosmetic only — never used in a join or branch (R7)
  first_seen, last_verified: turn_id
  interaction_count: int      # mechanically stamped: actions targeting this anchor
  rung: ANCHORED | ENGAGED | CHARACTERIZED     # computed (§2.3)
}

Locator (adapter-typed, pluggable):
  GridRegion{cells[], colors, bbox}      # ARC: connected components over raw integer grids
  ActionSlot{action_id: 1..7, param_schema}   # the fixed ARC action vocabulary
  TextSpan{doc_id, start, end}           # long-context instantiation (§7)

BindingRecord {               # append-only, Executive-written at EVERY B3 re-identification
  turn_id, component_hash,
  anchor_snapshot: {cells, bbox, signature},
  bound_to: R###, runner_up: R###?, margin: float
}                             # ConsequenceRecords key turn_id, so every receipt is
                              # deterministically joinable to the exact per-frame anchor
                              # it was observed under: identity assignments are replayable
                              # data, never destructive unions (§2.6)

ConsequenceRecord {           # the atom of grounding — unforgeable by construction
  turn_id, action, target_ref,
  predicted_delta,            # committed to Log BEFORE the action is emitted
  observed_delta,             # deterministic diff over raw integer grids
  match: bool,                # computed by the Executive, never by an LLM
  score_event, level_event, life_event    # signals the LLM cannot author
}

Relation {
  id, verb: {requires, enables, blocks, toggles, part_of, adjacent, same_as}
  src: R###, dst: R###
  claim: prose                # LLM hypothesis text
  status: HYPOTHESIS | TESTED | DEMOTED   # computed from ConsequenceRecords
  support: int, mismatch_count: int, contra_ttl: turn_id
  scope: WITHIN_LIFE | PERSISTENT | UNKNOWN
}

Rule {                        # mechanism fact — PROSE-WITH-SLOTS, never a program
  id, template: "WHEN <ctx_pred> DO <action, target=R###> THEN <effect_pattern>"
  ctx_pred: closed predicate over ARG records
  effect_pattern: ChangeSet pattern + score delta + state events
  status / support / counterexample_ptrs / scope / ttl   # as Relation; ttl MANDATORY on negatives
}

Goal { ... }                  # §3 — carries bindings: [R###], the alignment edge
Commitment { ... }            # §5 — the agenda; the only place decisions live;
                              # every step carries its compilation turn_id and its
                              # Executive-stamped premise closure (§5.3)
Procedure { ... }             # §5.4 — slotted, rebindable, scope-fingerprinted
Log { ... }                   # §4.4 — the ground the LLM cannot write
```

**Normative class definitions (used everywhere; nothing else counts as a "class").** A **referent class** is a machine-computed signature-equivalence class — the `signature` hash, nothing more. A **context class** is a deterministic partition minted by the Executive: when an action's `observed_delta` mismatches every existing receipt for that (action, context class) pair, the Executive mints a new context class from the ChangeSet partition — never LLM-minted, never LLM-named. Every use of "class" in this document (LEARN-* tests, ACTION6 sampling, Procedure scope fingerprints) resolves to one of these two definitions; no achievement test, sampling scheme, or scope fingerprint can gate on an LLM-inferred category (rule R7, §2.5).

The `scope` field enforces the invisible-state discipline: the environment holds state that never shows visually (an armed plate exists only behaviorally), so effects observed to survive a life-loss earn `PERSISTENT`, effects that vanish earn `WITHIN_LIFE`, default `UNKNOWN` until a death crosses them — and any plan chain crossing a `WITHIN_LIFE` link must compile within one life.

### 2.3 The grounding invariant — the claim scoped to what the mechanisms defend

> **Grounding claim, scoped.** In a formal environment, the world W is the environment's **state-transition function δ** (including invisible state), not the rendered frames. ARG's resolver ρ: R### → components-of-δ is **computed, never interpreted** — the engine adjudicates truth via Executive-verified prediction→outcome matches on signals the LLM cannot author (observed deltas, score/level/life events). ARG therefore claims **causal-informational grounding — Floridi's aspect (ii) — relative to this formal micro-world only.** It claims neither perceptual grounding (aspect i — no physical transduction is involved) nor normative-social grounding (aspect iii). The circumvention critique presupposes a W distinct from the system's representations; for a formal micro-world, δ is fully realized in the engine, so there is no further inaccessible W behind the transition function — the game *is* its engine. This is why grounding-by-consequences (the CHARACTERIZED tier) rather than grounding-by-appearances is the design's invariant: it satisfies a **consequence-verification criterion relative to a formal micro-world — deliberately weaker than Harnad's physical-transduction criterion, which is not claimed.** In the long-context instantiation (P3), the claim deflates further to **deterministic referential anchoring within a formal corpus** — no world-grounding is claimed there at all.

ARG accepts Floridi's diagnosis of LLM parasitism and is built as an implementation of his sanctioned route — curation, verification, and tooling as the legitimate means of soundly expanding capability without attributing understanding. Within ARG the LLM's parasitic linguistic competence is confined to where parasitism is harmless: labels, hypotheses, plans — all quarantined until receipted.

**The parasitism defeater is a stated condition of the evaluation, bound to three existing mechanisms.** (1) Hidden Phase-B games are novel artifacts with **no describing human corpus**, so referent-level competence cannot be inherited from pre-grounded human text about those referents — this is the anti-parasitism condition, not merely anti-memorization. (2) The LLM's parasitic competence enters only at the MENTIONED/HYPOTHESIS tiers, which cannot gate action until engine-receipted. (3) The §7 release gate certifies no task-isomorphic exemplars on any model-facing surface. Together: whatever referent competence the deployed system exhibits on Phase-B games was earned inside the loop, by receipts, or it does not exist.

Four tiers, every transition machine-verified:

- **MENTIONED (tier 0, quarantined).** A thing named only in LLM text is *not a referent*. It cannot appear in the roster, cannot be a plan target, cannot be a goal binding. It exists only inside hypothesis prose. This alone makes the dominant hallucination class inexpressible: you cannot intend toward, act on, or promote what was merely said.
- **ANCHORED (tier 1).** Exists as a deterministic extraction from raw sensor data. The LLM cannot mint, move, or delete an anchor. Tier 1 is also where salience bias dies at the root: *enumeration is salience-blind* — a 3-cell black plate and a 40-cell wall are created by the same code path and get identical records. Rhetoric demonstrably cannot buy this (61 declared targets under an explicit coverage instruction, 0 plate mentions with its coordinates on screen); segmentation buys it by construction.
- **ENGAGED (tier 2).** Has ≥1 ConsequenceRecord: an action was taken with this referent in scope and the observed ChangeSet — *including a null effect; "nothing changed" is evidence* — is logged with Log pointers.
- **CHARACTERIZED (tier 3).** Referenced by ≥1 TESTED Rule: a prediction *pre-registered before acting* was matched by the Executive. Appearance never grounds mechanism; only pre-committed prediction→observation matches do, because the LLM cannot author a match it did not pre-commit to.

Relations and Rules climb HYPOTHESIS → TESTED on `support ≥ k` receipts with mismatch ratio below threshold; mismatches beyond K demote back (never delete — the audit chain survives), auto-block dependent plan steps, and fire a Surveyor trigger. **Negative evidence decays on TTL**: one refused press must never permanently wall off a goal (the unroutable-winning-box failure); demoted records become re-probeable when their TTL expires. Beliefs decay toward doubt, not toward confidence.

The self-consistent hallucination cascade is thereby structurally impossible, not discouraged: the only path to TESTED runs through deterministic code, and the only inputs to that code are raw environment bytes and pre-registered predictions.

**Term discipline.** "Grounding," "belief," "hallucination cascade," and every mental-state predicate used anywhere in this design or the eventual paper are bound to mechanical definitions in the closed operational glossary enforced by the §7 release gate. The word "grounding" everywhere in this document, including metric names (RGR), denotes exactly the scoped claim of the box above and nothing stronger.

### 2.4 Size and decay discipline

The structure must be as consumable at beat 500 as at beat 5. **The render budget is the binding invariant; retention guarantees are scoped so they can never collide with it.**

1. **Working set vs archive.** Every consumer sees a bounded working set under the hard per-call render ceiling **B** (§2.4.7, rule R9); everything else lives in a queryable archive with a one-line mechanical index ("+N referents archived; recoverable by anchor or adjacency lookup, never by dump").
2. **Goal-binding retention — scoped to the active chain.** Only referents bound to the **currently active goal chain (root→leaf)** hold never-evicted status, regardless of dullness or recency. Bindings of other live goals render as compressed one-liners (`id | anchor | rung`) under mechanical rank. Retention-vs-budget precedence is resolved **at admission, not at render time**: gate 6 of §3.3 requires Σ|bindings| over all live goals ≤ N_max, where N_max is chosen so that the irreducible zones plus N_max fixed-width roster/goal-card rows ≤ B *by arithmetic*. A goal whose bindings would push the guaranteed-retained set past the Z2 sub-budget is inadmissible until the Surveyor retires goals or bindings through the existing gates — the admission gate returns a forced status decision (suspend the lowest-rank sibling), so the live-goal count is capped by the budget itself and the collision is inexpressible rather than unspecified. *(Resolution note: the goal-layer backpressure and the admission-arithmetic cap arrived as two round-1 amendments; they are one mechanism and are specified once, here and in §3.3 gate 6.)*
3. **Mechanical eviction rank** for everything else: f(link-to-active-goal-chain, recency-of-consequence, grounding rung). Stale referents go DORMANT — out of the render, never out of the store; any new evidence revives them.
4. **Merge, don't duplicate — and merge only above the margin.** Identity conflicts resolve via `same_as` edges (both ids preserved, one canonical). Bind-time merges below the assignment margin τ are forbidden outright (§2.6): the Executive mints NEW plus a `same_as` HYPOTHESIS edge instead, so every low-confidence merge is a testable, reversible belief by construction.
5. **Evidence compaction.** After n consistent receipts, older receipts compress to a count plus sampled exemplar Log pointers.
6. **Cross-level/game boundary.** Instances reset. Rules and Procedures survive only **demoted to HYPOTHESIS** with prior support noted — hypotheses to re-test cheaply, never facts to trust (the converting fix went 0/16 on the next level; "going on the hatch" can never fossilize). ARG transfers its way of binding, never its bindings.
7. **Per-organ budget table (renderer invariants — hard numbers, no tildes).** Before any scored run, a per-backbone-context-class table pins: the full-view ceiling **B** per LLM call; per-zone sub-caps Z1–Z6; **Z5 annotations limited to working-set members** (sharing Z2's cap); **Observer Log tail ≤ L turns** (pinned constant); **Surveyor read** = active chain root→leaf + gated PROPOSED frontier + top-K staleness/uncertainty-ranked slices, with all other ACCEPTED/REJECTED goals folded to a single mechanical count line with queryable ids ("+N ACCEPTED, +M REJECTED, recoverable by id") — the goal-layer analogue of evidence compaction and the direct equivalent of HIPIF's completed-progress folding. Overflow resolves by the mechanical eviction rank and is logged; **silent truncation is a Q11 legibility-harness failure**. **B is calibrated inside the measured flat region of each backbone's positional-bias curve** via a one-time position-sweep probe on the actual fixed template (needle-style referent-join queries at varying positions and prompt lengths — cheap because R6 fixes the template); the sweep and the chosen B are reported in the paper. Per-call prompt tokens are logged per organ every beat, with the pre-registered flatness invariant: **regression slope of tokens-vs-beat ≈ 0 after a stated warmup**; a breach prints a named Q13 diagnosis row. The tested size invariant: **max render tokens is constant in store size — O(working set), never O(history).**

### 2.5 Serialization contract — the join that defeats bias structurally

This is the load-bearing surface: the generalization of the single variable that flipped 0/200 → 3/3. Every deciding call sees a **fixed positional layout**, identical across turns, games, and environments:

| Zone | Content | Bias it defeats mechanically |
|---|---|---|
| **Z1 HEAD** | turn, level, score, lives; active goal chain root→leaf; **the active Commitment step verbatim** | goal drift — the commitment is the first thing read |
| **Z2 ROSTER** | exhaustive table, one fixed-width row per working-set referent, stable ID order: `id \| kind \| anchor \| rung \| interactions \| last consequence \| serves(goals)` | salience: a 3-cell plate gets exactly the row weight of a 200-cell wall; `interactions=0` is a printed, sortable column, not a hope |
| **Z3 RULES/RELATIONS** | TESTED first, then HYPOTHESIS (tagged), hard row cap (§2.4.7) | prose drift; dead-knowledge inertness |
| **Z4 UNTOUCHED FRONTIER** | mechanical enumeration of never-tried (referent × action) pairs in active goal scope | exploration re-measuring settled questions; novelty pressure is enumerated, never exhorted |
| **Z5 RENDER** | the grid render with **inline joins**: every region matching a **working-set** referent annotated `«R:id rung»` at its cells (join side A; cap shared with Z2) | the cross-referencing failure itself: prose-fact and render-pixel never need attention to join |
| **Z6 GOAL CARD** | active Goal statement + achievement test + status; **every referent bound to it**: id, anchor coordinates, render cross-reference, rung, one-line consequence signature (join side B); current Commitment step + predicted effect; unresolved mismatches | U-curve recency; intention–referent binding co-located at the privileged tail position |

Contract rules, each replacing a class of "pay attention to X" prose with a mechanism:

- **R1 — one row, one truth.** All joins are pre-computed by the Executive; no consumer ever joins across prompt regions. The failure class is removed, not overcome.
- **R2 — no naked mentions.** Any prose anywhere in a rendered prompt that mentions a referent is rewritten `label#R###` by string substitution against the alias table. Distance between mentions becomes irrelevant because every mention carries its own ground — the enforcement LINK-KG's prompt cache gestured at but confined to its coreference module; here it is inescapable at every occurrence in every prompt.
- **R3 — the join is bidirectional and redundant.** The same id and coordinates appear in Z5 (render side) and Z6 (goal side). Cross-reference requires *zero* long-range attention; the LONGPIBENCH spacing effect is collapsed to zero distance for everything that matters this turn.
- **R4 — position allocated by decision-criticality.** Primacy carries the commitment (Z1); recency carries the goal–referent join (Z6); the middle carries only corroboration — whose actual uptake is not assumed but **measured per zone by the ZCR canary below**. Any Z6 step re-cites by id whatever mid-context rows it depends on.
- **R5 — outputs cite ids or die.** Every LLM output field referring to a thing must cite an R###; dangling references are rejected on ingest under the §2.1 retry budget.
- **R6 — fixed template = learnable surface.** The layout never varies, so a mid-tier model amortizes layout comprehension and a small local model (the Kaggle target) can be lightly tuned on the layout itself. Zones are tiered for small-context fallback: Z1+Z6 irreducible; Z2 truncates by mechanical rank; Z3 to TESTED-only; Z5 falls back to coordinate-only joins. The fallback's **live trigger is ZCR zone-differential failure** (below), not only offline cost curves.
- **R7 — no branching on LLM-proposed annotations.** No Executive path — join, compilation, means analysis, eviction ranking, Z4 enumeration, ACTION6 sampling, or view construction — may branch on `kind`, `label`, or any LLM-proposed annotation. Branching is permitted only on id, Locator geometry, signature, rung, receipts, and goal bindings. **Q11 harness test:** randomize every referent's kind and label; all rendered views, AnswerViews, frontier enumerations, and compiled Commitments must be byte-identical modulo the cosmetic label text.
- **R8 — parameters are contained or die.** Every LLM-emitted concrete parameter (ACTION6 (x,y); TextSpan offsets in the P3 instantiation) must pass a mechanical Locator-containment check against the cited R###'s anchor (x,y ∈ anchor.cells; any enum/param must match the step's param_schema) **before emission**. Failure triggers one typed re-prompt that renders ONLY the target's roster row plus its joined render crop (distractor count ≈ 0 on retry); a second failure falls back deterministically (anchor centroid), stamped FALLBACK. A well-formed coordinate copied from an adjacent roster row is thereby a caught violation, not an admissible action. *(Resolution note: two round-1 amendments — the interference-substitution rule and the pre-emission hop validator — specify the same mechanism; merged here.)*
- **R9 — hard per-call render ceiling.** No LLM call under any organ contract ever receives more than **B** tokens of rendered view (§2.4.7). `render_tokens` is stamped per call into the TurnRecord; `render_tokens ≤ B` joins the Q11 per-beat harness, with breach ⇒ run invalid for attribution. Cost tables report the maximum render tokens ever observed per run. The Surveyor's input arrow is accordingly named **BudgetedView**; no organ in this design reads a "full view."

*(Numbering resolution: three round-1 amendments independently claimed the name "R7"; resolved here as R7 annotation-blind branching, R8 parameter containment, R9 render ceiling.)*

**Consumption canary — ZCR (zone consumption rate).** Content being *in* the view is necessary, not sufficient; uptake is measured, not assumed. At a fixed cadence (every k-th LLM call per organ contract, plus every Surveyor epoch), the Executive salts the rendered view with one synthetic, mechanically verifiable row per zone tier — a nonce-keyed referent row at a controlled Z2 position, a nonce rule in Z3, a nonce re-cite in Z6. Every organ output schema carries a mandatory `canary_echo` field (the nonce ids observed). Canary rows carry quarantined ids: never persisted, never joinable, never plan-targetable, excluded from all task metrics; cost is tens of tokens and zero game actions (internal operations are score-free per the verified ARC-AGI-3 methodology). The Executive computes per-zone echo rates as a Q13 meta-metric with a pre-registered floor. Diagnosis ordering is fixed: (1) uniform echo failure across zones → adapter/transport defect (Q11 class; run invalid for attribution); (2) zone-differential failure (middle zones drop first) → consumption rot — prints a named diagnosis row AND mechanically triggers R6's Z-tier fallback; (3) canaries clean + prediction mismatches → belief/reasoning error, routed to the Q5 repair path. **Calibration:** the §8 Z-tier sweep pre-registers the ZCR↔task-metric correlation (falsified-if: ZCR floors breach with no task-metric movement, or vice versa), so ZCR is a calibrated proxy before it is trusted in production.

### 2.6 Identity: bind, detect, fission — the repair lifecycle

Identity assignment is a forced choice executed every beat, so it needs the same detect-and-repair machinery as every other belief. Four pieces, all Executive-side, no new organ:

1. **BindingRecord (schema, §2.2).** Appended at every B3 re-identification: the per-frame anchor snapshot, the chosen id, the runner-up, and the assignment margin. Because ConsequenceRecords key `turn_id`, every receipt is deterministically joinable to the exact anchor it was observed under — identity assignments are replayable data, never destructive unions.
2. **Margin rule — uncertain merges become edges, not unions.** Bind-time merge into an existing id is permitted only when the assignment margin ≥ τ. Below τ, the Executive must mint NEW plus a `same_as` HYPOTHESIS Relation, which climbs or demotes under the standard receipt lifecycle — so every low-confidence merge (exactly where vague-alias cache-poisoning lives) is a testable, reversible belief by construction.
3. **Fission detector (mechanical, never a judgment).** Extending B4's counterexample buckets: the Executive fires FISSION-CHECK when a referent's mismatches exceed K **and** the match/mismatch partition correlates with a binding feature (signature variant, spatial cluster, or temporal segment) above a pre-registered threshold — a computed statistic over BindingRecords.
4. **FISSION operation (Executive-executed, incremental).** Re-cluster that referent's BindingRecords by signature × spatio-temporal continuity; mint fresh ids per cluster; **re-attribute every ConsequenceRecord** via its turn_id-joined BindingRecord; recompute rungs and Rule support/mismatch per new id; demote all Rules/Relations citing the old id to HYPOTHESIS; retire the old id with `split_into` edges (never delete — the audit chain survives); widen Goal.bindings to all children; dependent Commitment steps auto-block via the existing demotion mechanism; fire T1. Cost is bounded by the one referent's receipt set — an O(interaction_count) incremental lifecycle transition, never an offline re-pass.

Metrics: **FSN** (fissions per 100 actions) and **post-fission false-TESTED delta** join §8. The residual risk statement moves to §9.1 in its corrected, narrower form.

## 3. Multi-Horizon Goals

Goals are **expandable axioms** — and they are themselves referents whose anchor is their achievement test. Goal drift is thereby **measured through the same referent machinery** that measures grounding; whether its binding component *is* the same failure as referent-grounding failure is the P2/X1/X2 experimental outcome (§1, §8), not a definition. This layer fills the gap a curriculum cannot: curriculum steers what gets *learned* (every model completed Sensi's curriculum; completions stayed at zero), while the goal ladder relates structural learnings to ordered ends and knows, mechanically, when each end is achieved — which is what drives *doing*.

### 3.1 Schema

```
Goal {
  id: G###, parent: G###?,
  rank: int                                 # orders siblings; later ranks depend on earlier.
                                            # EXECUTIVE-COMPUTED (§5.3): deterministic function of
                                            # dependency edges, status-machine state, and remaining
                                            # budget — never LLM-written; Surveyor proposals survive
                                            # only as Revision-Evidence-gated tie-breakers
  statement: prose                          # LLM/human readable
  bindings: [R###]                          # the referents this goal is ABOUT — the alignment edge
  achievement_test: closed predicate over Log events + ARG records
                                            # mechanically evaluable; IMMUTABLE after admission
  discriminator: ChangeSet/event pattern    # what observable differs between achieved / not
  status: PROPOSED | EXPLORED | VALIDATED | REJECTED | ACCEPTED | REOPENED   # computed
  rejection_reason: enum?                   # stamped (e.g. NEVER_VALIDATED), machine-set
  budget: {actions, search_calls}           # bounded pursuit
  provenance: SEEDED | epoch_id | HUMAN     # stamped
  relevance_edges: [rule_id | R###]         # stamped as a side effect of compilation (§4.3)
}
```

**Achievement tests are consequence-based, never appearance-based, and declared only against the adapter's verified signal vocabulary.** The adapter enumerates observable signals with their empirically observed transitions *before* any test may reference them; a test referencing an unverified signal is inadmissible. This kills the deadlock class by construction: a gate keyed on `GameState.WIN` while the environment reported level advances as `NOT_FINISHED` + `levels_completed++` stranded 5/5 recorded wins behind a circular gate.

### 3.2 Pre-seeded axioms (game-agnostic)

One seeded root plus generic expansion *templates* — no game nouns anywhere:

- **G0 "win the game"** — test keyed **disjunctively on all progress signals** (`levels_completed` increment OR win state OR score event), with a **test-the-test audit**: when any progress signal fires, verify the others' behavior and repair the predicate.
- Template **LEARN-ACTIONS** — test: every action in the fixed vocabulary (ACTION1–5, 7; ACTION6(x,y) sampled over anchored **referent classes** — signature-equivalence classes per §2.2, never inferred categories) has ≥1 ConsequenceRecord — effect *or verified no-op* — in **every live context class** (context classes are Executive-minted per §2.2; a freshly minted class flips this universal test false automatically, which is what reopens the goal — §3.4).
- Template **LEARN-RULES** — test: every referent class with an observed change participates in ≥1 TESTED Rule, and window contradiction rate < θ.
- Template **LEARN-ENV** — test: every ANCHORED referent has interactions ≥ 1 or is unreachable by grounded means.

The templates are the *optional curriculum* — deletable as an ablation arm (§8, A6), human-adjustable in early versions through the same admission gate (provenance-stamped HUMAN), and scrubbed of anything game-isomorphic as a release gate (one human exemplar on a model-facing surface voids the generality claim even with zero behavioral echo). Expansions are *derived* per game, not hard-coded — the expected first expansion matches the owner's example (learn actions → learn rules → maybe learn env → win) but may legitimately differ.

### 3.3 Expansion — gating hallucinated intentions (the BDI option-generation gap)

The 2025 BDI literature names the open gap: essentially no principled ML-driven option generation, because generated intentions confabulate. ARG's answer: **an intention is admissible exactly when it is checkable** — inadmissible intentions are made inexpressible, not discouraged. The Surveyor proposes `Goal(PROPOSED)`; the Executive admits it only if all gates pass:

1. **Referential closure** — every id in `bindings` resolves to an ANCHORED-or-better referent. You cannot intend toward a MENTIONED thing; "insert a coin" is inadmissible until a coin-like referent is anchored (the confident false coin-slot theory becomes unwritable as a goal, not just doubted).
2. **Mechanical test** — `achievement_test` compiles in the closed predicate vocabulary over non-hallucinable events, is evaluable now (returns false, not error), **and evaluates FALSE at admission** *(amendment A3, adopted 2026-07-17)*: a test already true when proposed is a zero-information goal — achievement without work — and is rejected as `GATE2_TRUE_AT_ADMISSION`. Batch-4 receipts: three "confirm RU0011 is demoted" goals auto-ACCEPTED the beat they were admitted; the record they "achieved" already existed. The Surveyor's correct move is citing that record as *evidence* for its next goal, not re-stating it as an achievement. "Understand the door" is inadmissible; "ACTION2 on R14 produces a diff in R14 on 2 consecutive trials" is admissible.
3. **Discriminator** — an observable consequence pattern distinguishing achieved from not. Appearance-based tests are rejected by grammar.
4. **Non-duplication + anti-oscillation** — no live sibling with an equivalent test; and **re-proposal of a goal whose prior version was REJECTED as NEVER_VALIDATED must cite at least one evidence record with `turn_id` greater than the rejection's `turn_id`** — otherwise inadmissible even after the rejection's TTL expires. A confabulation cannot be re-minted on a fresh budget without new evidence post-dating its disproof. *(This new-evidence-or-inadmissible mechanism is reused verbatim as the §5.3 Revision-Evidence gate on agenda ops.)*
5. **Provenance + budget** — cites ≥1 evidence record as motivation and carries a bounded budget; exhaustion forces the Executive-computed terminal rule of §3.4 (never a discretionary decision), so the tree cannot accrete zombie intentions.
6. **Binding-budget admission (the retention–budget precedence rule, §2.4.2).** Σ|bindings| over all live goals ≤ N_max, where N_max is fixed by arithmetic so that the irreducible zones plus N_max fixed-width rows fit under the render ceiling B. A goal whose bindings would breach the cap is inadmissible until goals or bindings are retired; the gate's forced alternative is an explicit suspension of the lowest-rank sibling. The live-goal count is capped by the budget itself.

### 3.4 Status machine (all transitions Executive-computed)

- **PROPOSED → EXPLORED**: gates pass and the first Commitment step serving this goal is consumed — an action was actually spent on it. **PROPOSED → REJECTED**: gate failure, reason stored, re-proposable under the anti-oscillation rule when evidence changes.
- **EXPLORED → VALIDATED**: the goal is confirmed *real* — its discriminator has been observed to vary, or a TESTED Rule links it to its parent's test. "The path is real; the work remains."
- **EXPLORED/VALIDATED → REJECTED**: a premise Relation was demoted, the discriminator shown moot, or the parent achieved without it. **Terminal rule on budget exhaustion (Executive-computed, no organ judgment):** any goal with status below VALIDATED — its discriminator never observed to vary AND no TESTED Rule linking it to its parent's test — is auto-REJECTED with stamped reason **NEVER_VALIDATED**, *regardless of effect-row count*. An anchored, reactive, task-irrelevant decoy that accrues effect rows therefore cannot immortalize its goal: reactivity is not validation, and the organ that minted the goal never adjudicates it. Rejection keeps its evidence and decays on TTL — subject to gate 4's new-evidence requirement on re-proposal.
- **→ ACCEPTED**: `achievement_test` fired on Log events, evaluated by the Executive every beat — achievement detection costs zero LLM calls, cannot be missed, and cannot be declared (an LLM saying "done" is not an event).
- **ACCEPTED → REOPENED (the monotonicity rule).** B6 continues evaluating achievement tests **after** acceptance, at zero cost. Tests keyed solely on monotone Log events (score/level/win increments — events that cannot un-happen) remain terminal once fired. Any test that **quantifies over ARG records** — all LEARN-* templates — transitions ACCEPTED → REOPENED the beat it evaluates false: audit chain preserved, a T-trigger fired, and the goal re-enters the Actuator's rank walk ahead of its dependents. Compiled steps of dependent goals auto-block on a REOPENED predecessor exactly as they do on a DEMOTED relation. Combined with Executive context-class minting (§2.2), the classic false closure repairs itself with zero LLM involvement: an action whose delta mismatches every receipt in its (action, context-class) pair mints a new context class; the new class flips the LEARN-ACTIONS universal test false; the goal reopens mechanically. A WITHIN_LIFE-scoped goal additionally reopens on death, as before.

`achievement_test` is immutable after admission: revising a goal creates a new versioned Goal with provenance, and the old one is REJECTED with reason. The agent can change its mind — never silently, and never by weakening a test it is failing. ACCEPTED goals remain queryable ("achieved when, by what receipts"), and `relevance_edges` — stamped whenever a Rule or Referent is used in compilation for a goal (§4.3) — make "relating structural learnings to ordered goals" a mechanical by-product of doing, not a bookkeeping duty. Goal drift becomes a measured structural quantity (§8: GDS-bind, GDS-abandon, GA, APR, FCR): every action either descends from a live goal's Commitment or it does not, and every closure is re-checked for as long as its subject matter can change.

### 3.5 The goal chain — sufficiency recognizer, verified fill, milestones, deficits *(adopted 2026-07-17; amendments A1/A2; live-validated batch 4)*

The owner's load-bearing question — *"do we have enough goals to implement the multi-step mechanic, or must we create more?"* — is answered mechanically, every beat, by a deterministic records-only computation. No LLM appears anywhere inside it.

**The recognizer.** `chain_status(G) ∈ {COMPILABLE, REDUCIBLE, DEFICIT(holes)}`, bottom-up: a goal is COMPILABLE when means analysis serves it *now* (a TESTED rule, an applicable Procedure, or a milestone's named rule — below); REDUCIBLE when every unmet effect-need is covered by a **verified, non-DEFICIT child** along dependency edges (record-quantified pursuits with no effect keys — the LEARN class — reduce via the probe fallback and never block the chain); DEFICIT otherwise, where each hole is **typed and carries its own derivable evidence**: the non-DEMOTED rules whose closed effect bears the missing key (the auto-fill candidates) and whether an action model exists (can we even navigate). "Enough goals exist" ⇔ `holes = ∅`. Terminal children contribute nothing: an ACCEPTED goal is trivially COMPILABLE; a REJECTED (NEVER_VALIDATED) child is terminal DEFICIT and **stops covering its parent** — budget exhaustion re-opens the chain honestly. The zero-knowledge state needs no special mode: an empty store is total DEFICIT with empty-evidence holes, and that *is* the explore state (pinned as the recognizer's first test; observed live on every generative cold start).

**Dependency edges with verified fill.** `goal_edge{parent, child, hole, verified}` (append-only). An edge means *child fills a named hole of parent*, and `verified=1` is set only by the Executive's mechanical proof — two checkable forms: **knowledge fill** (the child's test names a rule — `RULE_STATUS(RU, TESTED)` or `EXISTS/COUNT consequence{predictor: RU, match: 1}` — whose closed effect covers a parent need) or **signal fill** (the child's own effect-keys ⊆ the parent's unmet needs). A Surveyor proposal may *claim* a fill (`fills_hole`); the claim is proven or the edge stays unverified — admitted goals cannot fake chain completeness. Verified edges feed §5.3's rank key #1: children order before the parents they serve.

**Knowledge milestones (amendment A1 — the sub-goal vocabulary).** Intermediate goals stay consequence-grounded by being milestones *of knowledge*, not of appearance: closed `where`-clauses over Executive-stamped fields only — `EXISTS/COUNT consequence{action, target, match, predictor, score_event, level_event, context_class}`, `COUNT rule{status}`, `COUNT referent{rung}`, `RULE_STATUS(RU, is)`, `RUNG(R, at_least)`. "Deliberately test RU####" := `COUNT consequence{predictor: RU, match: 1} ≥ k` where k is the promotion bar (K_SUPPORT) — achieving the milestone makes the parent's means analysis succeed *by construction*. Nothing appearance-based can enter through a where-clause; the §7 vocabulary lint covers the extension.

**Milestone compilation = navigate-then-interact, emergent.** A milestone whose named rule is non-DEMOTED **and exercisable** compiles as an *experiment-commitment while the rule is still HYPOTHESIS*: NAVIGATE\* + INTERACT at the rule's (action, target), the rule's closed effect pre-registered as the step prediction, the rule itself as step premise (auto-block on demotion). B4a additionally pre-registers the rule via `applicable_rules`, so executing the milestone mints exactly the receipts its own test quantifies over. *Exercisable* (batch-4 amendment): a target-scoped rule reached via an untargeted action requires a navigate route now — an untargeted emission stamps `target=NULL`, so the rule's receipt could never land; compiling it anyway is a blind-commitment loop (gen4: 336 wasted beats). **Milestone budgets scale with the promotion bar by test *shape*, not provenance** — `max(8, 6·K_SUPPORT)` — a milestone is an experiment, not a campaign; exhaustion → NEVER_VALIDATED → the chain re-opens (§3.4's terminal rule doing chain duty).

**Two-tier expansion.** (1) **Deterministic auto-fill (amendment A2, 0 LLM):** a hole that names its own fill — HYPOTHESIS rules carrying the needed effect — is derivable; the Executive drafts the milestone sub-goals itself, admits them through the *same six gates*, provenance `DEFICIT`, and creates the verified edge. (2) **Deficit-driven Surveyor expansion:** non-derivable holes are STAMPED (append-only `deficit_stamp`, deduped per goal) and rendered in the epoch view's **DEFICIT block** with hole ids — the machine-stated question. The Surveyor answers with `fills_hole` proposals; gate-checked, fill-proven. The stamps are the auditable record that the system *knew* its chain was incomplete, where, and with what evidence — replayable per turn.

**The walk is chain-aware (§4.3 amendment).** Per unachieved goal in rank order: compile directly (procedures → TESTED means → milestone); else execute the **chain frontier** — the deepest compilable goal along verified edges; else, on DEFICIT, auto-fill and compile the fill; a residue of non-derivable holes stamps. Walk candidates include the chain **ancestors deepest-first** — the root's own deficit stays visible while curriculum children live (without this the recognizer is never consulted for the goal that matters). Consume-on-success propagates upward: a child's ACCEPT re-evaluates the parent's holes the same beat.

**Generative curriculum (the cold-start default).** With `ARG_SEEDS=0` the store opens with G0 alone; the first beat stamps total DEFICIT; the first epoch's DEFICIT block *is* the curriculum brief. Batch-4 receipts: zero-seed Sonnet runs generated the canonical learn-shaped chain ("Discover an action that produces a level-completion event / a score-increasing event") from the deficit brief alone; seeded LEARN-\* templates become the A6 ablation arm. What no gate can conjure is *feedstock*: the chain engages only where some organ hypothesizes an effect-linked rule — the pre-registered 4d lever (campaign doc) invites `PROPOSE_RULE` on empty-evidence holes.

## 4. The Organs and Their APIs

Three LLM **role contracts** (Observer, Surveyor, Actuator) around one store, plus two pieces of deterministic code: the **Executive** (differ, matcher, validator, compiler, pather, renderer, test-evaluator, fission operator) and the **Log**. *(Naming: the search organ is the **Surveyor** — the owner's brief posed its name as an open question ("called 'conscious'?"), and this design resolves that question with an operational name matching the organ's function: the separate search mechanism that expands referent grounding into a live model + agenda. See Appendix 13.)* The roles are hard-isolated — no shared context window; the rendered views are the only shared state — but they run on **one backbone by default**; splitting them across distinct models is an ablation arm (§8, A8), not a baseline assumption. This keeps the reviewer-mandated backbone-swap matrix small (swap one model, not three) and makes role-separation itself a measured claim.

Topology (every arrow schema-validated and byte-tested):

```
Actuator --(TurnRecord)--> Log --(TurnBundle)--> Observer --(typed deltas)--> ARG
Actuator <--(AnswerView)-- ARG <--(gated proposals)-- Surveyor <--(BudgetedView)-- ARG
```

Log is a **directed bridge, Actuator → Observer only**: the Observer reads Log to know what was just done and predicted (attribution by `turn_id` keying kills the off-by-one class); the Actuator never reads Log — it reads ARG views only, keeping raw history out of decisions and ARG the single control plane (the database-as-control-plane pattern, retained deliberately). Perception is never contaminated by intention: the Observer never sees Actuator reasoning, only its logged actions and pre-committed predictions, so prediction-matching cannot become self-confirming.

### 4.1 Observer — percepts → structure (answers open question 1)

**Runs only when needed:** on a beat whose deterministic diff is non-trivial or whose prediction mismatched. Settled transitions cost zero LLM calls. Perception is **programmatic-first**: the Executive computes the ChangeSet over raw integer grids and extracts anchors before any LLM sees anything; the LLM only interprets.

**Input (typed):** `{changeset, Log tail keyed by turn_id, length ≤ L (§2.4.7), incl. last action + its pre-committed prediction, Z1+Z2+Z5 view}`. Vision paths carry a startup image-receipt round-trip test — verify the model received pixels, byte-level, before any run counts.

**Output — a closed algebra of typed deltas over pre-anchored identities:**

```
ObservationDelta ::= BIND(component_hash → R### | NEW)     # forced choice among Executive-proposed
                                                           # candidates (anchor-overlap + signature,
                                                           # Hungarian assignment); merge permitted
                                                           # only at margin ≥ τ, else NEW + same_as
                                                           # HYPOTHESIS (§2.6); the LLM labels,
                                                           # it never invents an anchor
                   | NOTE_EVENT(R###, event_kind, turn_id)
                   | PROPOSE_RELATION(verb, src, dst, evidence_ptr)
                   | PROPOSE_RULE(template, ctx, effect, scope=UNKNOWN)
                   | INTERPRET(R###, label)                # cosmetic only (R7: never branched on)
                   | ANNOTATE(free_text)                   # quarantined: never joins, never compiles
```

The validator rejects any op referencing an entity or coordinate absent from the ChangeSet or roster, and — per the §2.1 completeness invariant — rejects any response lacking a verdict for **every** Executive-proposed `component_hash`; silence on a candidate is a violation, not a skip. All rejections run under the §2.1 retry budget and are metered as WER. The Observer's output schema carries the mandatory `canary_echo` field (§2.5 ZCR). `match` on ConsequenceRecords is computed by the Executive comparing the *pre-committed* prediction to the raw diff; any Observer opinion about matching is recorded but weightless (self-reports are gameable; only mechanical stamps form a floor).

**Q1 answered.** The symbolic relation that turns Observer output into a grounding-safe structure is **anchored delta interpretation**: percept → machine-computed anchor → persistent id → typed op → appended receipt. The symbol's denotation is computed, not asserted; the LLM contributes only the mapping from anchored change to semantic hypothesis. This is exactly what LINK-KG's alias cache (text grounded in other text), BDI belief bases (asserted, not receipted), and prior prose fact stores (coordinates that never joined to the render) each lacked: the referent is *born* from the raw data path and never separates from it.

### 4.2 Surveyor — the separate search mechanism (owner need 3)

The organ that reasons over the referent structure and turns it into a **live model + agenda** — externalizing what a frontier model at high effort holds in attention. Deliberately off the action path.

**Triggers (never a timer):** **T1** contradiction — a TESTED Rule's prediction failed, or a FISSION fired (§2.6) (fires immediately, overrides everything); **T2** goal transition — including ACCEPTED → REOPENED (§3.4); **T3** compilation failure — the Actuator's query could not be answered; **T4** stall — S beats with no new evidence row, with §2.1 unexplained-ChangeSet flags counted as stall input (default S=12); **T5** boundary — level/game transition (runs the demotion + re-seeding pass). Rate limit: one epoch per C beats (default C=10) except T1.

**Reads: the BudgetedView** — active goal chain root→leaf, gated PROPOSED frontier, and top-K staleness/uncertainty-ranked slices of the goal tree and archive index, under the same hard ceiling B as every other organ (§2.4.7, R9); all other ACCEPTED/REJECTED goals appear as a single mechanical count line with queryable ids; the archive is reachable only by keyed lookup. Sorting by staleness/uncertainty lands attention on the frontier of ignorance, not on settled questions. **Writes (all through gates, all PROPOSED-on-entry):** goal expansions (§3.3 gates); Relation/Rule hypotheses *with test plans*; **Experiments** — probes with pre-registered `predicted_delta` and a `discriminates: [rule_ids]` field forcing probes that can separate rival hypotheses (the structural defense against consistent-but-wrong theories); **Commitment revisions — `AbortStep(step_id, evidence_ptr)`, admissible only through the §5.3 Revision-Evidence gate**; **rank tie-break proposals under the identical gate** (rank itself is Executive-computed — §5.3). **The Surveyor can never write a fact, promote a rule, mark a goal achieved, touch an anchor, or flip agenda state without citing qualifying new evidence.** The worst an unsupported Surveyor hypothesis can do is spend bounded probes efficiently disproving itself.

**Budget:** ≤3 calls per epoch, hard per-level epoch cap scaling with level index, all calls and tokens metered (`search_calls`). Rule induction **hill-climbs on best-so-far with mechanical counterexample buckets** — never from scratch, feedback never interpreted (from-scratch proposal collapsed 0.94 → 0.67 → 0.54; anchored hill-climbing with mechanical buckets recovered it).

### 4.3 Actuator — query protocol (answers open question 2)

**Default path — not an LLM.** If a Commitment has a live step, the Executive executes it at API speed with zero calls: navigation and routing are deterministic and solved (a route plan delivered the agent to the winning referent at t10, 8× faster than frontier reasoning; the bottleneck is grounding and commitment, never movement). Every NAVIGATE step carries an **arrival handoff**: arrival is a consume-event that chains mechanically into an explicit `then:` interaction slot — reaching a referent and interacting with it are separate phases whose handoff must be mechanical (the fix that created post-arrival testing at all).

**The query.** "To achieve goal_x, what must I do?" is answered by **mechanical compilation, LLM-last**:

1. Evaluate goal_x's test. Satisfied → `ACHIEVED`; the Actuator walks to the next unachieved goal by rank — "nearest unachieved goal" is a tree walk over an Executive-computed order (§5.3), not a judgment; REOPENED goals re-enter the walk ahead of their dependents (§3.4).
2. **Means analysis over the Rule store:** find TESTED Rules whose effect_pattern advances the test's unmet predicates. (Each Rule/Referent used stamps `relevance_edges` — alignment as a by-product of doing.)
3. Chain found → compile to Commitment steps; **each step is stamped at compile time with its compilation `turn_id` and its premise closure — the machine-recorded set {cited rule ids ∪ goal bindings ∪ target refs} (§5.3 consumes both)**; preconditions are closed predicates over rungs and receipts, so a step resting on a DEMOTED relation or a REOPENED predecessor blocks automatically — the agent cannot act on a dead belief.
4. Unmet preconditions → each becomes a candidate sub-goal surfaced to the Surveyor through the §3.3 gate.
5. No applicable Rules → return the cheapest untested (referent × action) probe from Z4 in goal scope. **Under ignorance the answer is always a probe — never idleness, never a guess.**

The response is the **AnswerView**: a §2.5-contract render whose Z6 goal card carries the current step imperatively with every dependency re-cited by id, plus the predicted effect. Q2's answer: *alignment between structure and goals is not a search the LLM performs at query time — it is materialized at write time by `bindings` and `relevance_edges`, so answering is a computed view, not a generation.*

**LLM path (exception, ≤1 call at commitment boundaries):** when a step underdetermines the concrete command (e.g., choosing ACTION6 coordinates within a target anchor), the Actuator contract is invoked with the AnswerView. Its discretion is confined to parameter realization, and "confined" is a validator, not an adjective — **rule R8**: the realized parameter must pass the mechanical containment check (x,y ∈ the cited anchor's cell set; enum/param matching the step's `param_schema`) before emission; violation triggers one typed re-prompt rendering only the target's roster row plus its joined render crop, then deterministic fallback (anchor centroid) stamped FALLBACK. It cannot select goals and cannot skip steps.

**After acting:** the Actuator writes the TurnRecord to Log — with `predicted_delta` committed *before* emission. **`target_ref` is stamped by the Executive via containment lookup of the actually-emitted coordinates — never copied from the commitment step.** A step/landing disagreement is logged as a `SUBSTITUTION_CAUGHT` event and produces **no ConsequenceRecord against the step's target**, making misattributed receipts — and the false demotion of true Rules by interference errors — structurally impossible. The step advances only on Executive-confirmed match (consume-on-success). Every emitted action carries `source_stamp ∈ {COMMITMENT_STEP, PROBE, ACTUATOR_LLM, FALLBACK}`, stamped by the executing code path, never self-reported — the mechanical provenance floor for all attribution (declared-intention→plan conversions went 0 → 21 only when this chain became mechanical; a substring self-report channel is gameable by construction).

### 4.4 Log

```
TurnRecord { turn_id, action, params, target_ref,          # target_ref: Executive-stamped by
                                                           # containment lookup of emitted params
             pre_frame_hash, post_frame_hash,
             raw_diff, predicted_delta, observed_delta, match,
             score, level_counter, state_flags, lives,
             commitment_step_id, source_stamp, render_tokens,   # render_tokens per R9
             shadow_step_id?, drift_ref }                  # §8 shadow-agenda instrumentation:
                                                           # shadow_step_id stamped per beat in
                                                           # agenda-off cells; drift_ref ∈ {LIVE, SHADOW}
                                                           # names the reference step for drift metrics

WRITE_REJECT { organ, op_type, violation_class, retry_count, turn_id }   # §2.1
SUBSTITUTION_CAUGHT { turn_id, step_target, landed_target }              # §4.3
REVISION { step_id, evidence_ptr, turn_id }                # §5.3: every accepted agenda revision —
                                                           # replayable; the abort audit chain
```

Append-only, keyed by `turn_id`; the sole source of ConsequenceRecord payloads, the sole ground of achievement tests, and the substrate of every metric and ablation readout — including WER/DPR, ECR, SCL, SRR, FSN, FCR, APR, ZCR, and DRIFT_total. BindingRecords (§2.2/§2.6) sit beside it, joinable by `turn_id`. Frame hashing doubles as the loop-closure check that differ and Log agree byte-for-byte. It is the store no LLM can write — the anchor that makes every belief bottom out in environment bytes. Per the verified ARC-AGI-3 methodology, everything in it except emitted game commands is score-free ("internal operations... are not counted as actions"); token cost is fully reported regardless.

## 5. The Doing Loop

### 5.1 Two cadences

**Beat** (per emitted environment action):

```
B1  Actuator: live Commitment step? → execute deterministically [0 calls]
              else → query walk (§4.3) [0–1 calls, R8-validated]
B2  Environment responds; TurnRecord appended (prediction was committed pre-emission;
      target_ref Executive-stamped by containment lookup)
B3  Executive: deterministic diff; anchors updated; re-identification by signature;
      BindingRecord appended per assignment (margin, runner-up); sub-τ merges
      forbidden → NEW + same_as HYPOTHESIS (§2.6)
B4  Predicted-vs-observed check against the live step and all in-scope TESTED Rules
      match    → support++, cursor advances (consume-on-success)  [0 calls]
      mismatch → counterexample bucket++, T1 armed;
                 FISSION-CHECK statistic updated over BindingRecords (§2.6)
B5  Observer runs IFF the ChangeSet is unexplained  [0–1 calls, §2.1 retry budget]
B6  Typed deltas validated (completeness invariant) and applied; rungs recomputed;
      achievement tests evaluated — INCLUDING post-acceptance monotonicity checks (§3.4)
B7  Epoch triggers checked (T1–T5, rate-limited); FISSION executed if fired
B8  Views re-rendered incrementally (dirty zones only); render_tokens stamped;
      ZCR canaries salted at cadence; in agenda-off cells the shadow step is
      recompiled and shadow_step_id/drift_ref stamped (§8) — never rendered
```

**Epoch** (triggered, never scheduled): one budgeted Surveyor pass (§4.2).

Steady state: the modal beat costs **zero LLM calls**; the typical beat at most one. Score-relevant cost (emitted actions — which the official formula punishes quadratically) and LLM cost are decoupled and separately metered (zero-LLM execution previously cut cost ~5× with no knowledge-quality loss; calls are reserved for binding, induction, and parameter realization — the three places only LLMs help).

### 5.2 Where DOING dominates

There is no curriculum organ. What a curriculum bought is bought structurally: Z4's frontier enumeration supplies novelty pressure; LEARN-* goals with mechanical tests supply direction; probes are the default answer under ignorance. Every beat emits an action or advances a step. Learning goals are discharged *by doing* — their agendas are experiment sequences that spend actions. A curriculum *may* be injected as extra seeded goals through the same gate — optional by construction, exactly as specified ("we can use curriculum ...").

### 5.3 The agenda persists — nothing re-decides from scratch, and nothing revises without evidence

The Commitment record is the agenda, and it is the only place decisions live.

**The commitment policy, named:** ARG's policy is **single-minded in the BDI sense with mechanically monitored rationale** — a Commitment step persists until consumed, its premise demotes (auto-block → T1), or its lease expires; reconsideration is event-triggered (T1–T5), never per-beat, while per-beat monitoring (B4) is free deterministic code. This is the cheap-monitoring/dear-deliberation operating point the Kinny–Georgeff boldness experiments recommend, not 1991 blind commitment — and both extremes are measured, not asserted: A2-class re-deciding (the factorial's agenda-off cells) instruments thrash, arm A9 (`ARG_TRIGGERS=0`) instruments ossification, and **SCL** (stale-commitment latency: beats from a step's premise demotion, or a T5 demotion pass, to agenda revision) is the Log-stamped readout for both (§8).

- **The Actuator cannot abandon, reorder, or skip steps** — it can only consume-on-success or fail-with-observation. **Only the Surveyor can revise the agenda, and only through the Revision-Evidence gate below**; a bounded lease (max beats per step) backstops runaway steps, with lease expiry itself both a T-trigger and a qualifying evidence record for the gate, so commitment cannot protect a dead plan forever.

**Revision-Evidence gate (Executive-enforced; the §3.3 gate-4 mechanism reused on the only two LLM-writable agenda ops — no new machinery).** Every Commitment step carries, from compilation (§4.3 step 3), its compilation `turn_id` and its Executive-stamped **premise closure**: the set {cited rule ids ∪ goal bindings ∪ target refs}. The gate consumes both:

- **AbortStep(step_id, evidence_ptr)** is admissible iff the Executive verifies all three clauses: **(a) contradiction-class** — `evidence_ptr` resolves to an Executive-stamped record in the closed class {ConsequenceRecord with `match=false`, demotion event, FISSION, REOPENED transition, lease expiry}; any other id, however resolvable, is rejected — a merely-citable record is not a contradiction; **(b) fresh** — `evidence.turn_id` > the step's compilation `turn_id`; **(c) relevant** — the evidence's subject (its `target_ref`, rule id, or goal id) intersects the step's premise closure. Violations reject under the §2.1 retry budget and meter into WER. Each **accepted** abort appends a replayable **REVISION** record `{step_id, evidence_ptr, turn_id}` to Log (§4.4) — the same auditability every grounding transition already has. Clauses (b) and (c) are what keep B4's routinely accumulating sub-threshold mismatch buckets from serving as a universal abort token: stale or unrelated mismatches do not qualify, so a stochastically re-reading Surveyor has no always-available id to "cite."
- **Rank is Executive-computed, not LLM-owned.** `Goal.rank` is a deterministic function recomputed by the Executive on every status or budget transition: dependency edges first — *implemented (§3.5): a goal with a VERIFIED `goal_edge` into a live parent orders ahead of its siblings; children before the parents they serve* — then status-machine precedence (REOPENED ahead of dependents, per §3.4), then remaining budget, then id as the final total-order tiebreak. Surveyor rank proposals survive only as **tie-breakers among siblings the computation leaves equal** — and even a tie-break proposal must pass the identical Revision-Evidence gate: cited qualifying evidence with `turn_id` greater than the current order's set-at turn, subject intersecting the affected goals' bindings. *(Resolution note: two admissible variants were on the table — gate the LLM-owned rank edit, or compute rank outright. Resolved by composing them: computation removes the load-bearing lever entirely, and the gate covers the residual tie-break channel, so no evidence-free path to pursuit order exists in either half. §4.3's "tree walk, not a judgment" is thereby literal: the walk order is Executive state.)*
- **SRR — spurious-revision rate** (gate-rejected AbortStep/rank proposals per Surveyor epoch) joins the §8 metric table with a pre-registered ceiling: sustained gate pressure is a printed diagnosis of an unstable Surveyor, never a silent battle between organ and gate.

With this gate, every agenda transition — like every grounding-rung and goal-status transition already — can be flipped **only by new Executive-verified evidence**. A re-reading, however stochastic, has no load-bearing state left to act on: referents, rules, goal status, and now abort and rank are all either Executive-computed or new-evidence-gated.

- **Surfaced at both basin ends every turn:** Z1's first line and Z6's card (e.g., `ACTIVE: step 2/3 of G4 — INTERACT R7 (3c black, r21c31, visible in RENDER)`). Arrivals and commitments invisible to cognition were a named defect until rendered explicitly.
- **Survives everything:** store state, not context state — context resets, model swaps, process restarts, and (for PERSISTENT-scoped chains) deaths.
- The externalized self-commitment: a frontier model won by writing and obeying its own commitment for 7 turns; a mid-tier model wrote the winning move verbatim twice and executed it zero times across 171 consecutive un-committed turns. Under this loop that sentence becomes a Commitment at the turn it is written and is the rendered instruction from the next beat until consumed. The machinery *performs* for mid-tier what rhetoric demonstrably cannot request.

### 5.4 Discovered actions and procedures

A Commitment whose steps all confirmed distills into a `ProcedureTemplate`: ordered `(action_kind, referent_role_slot, expected_delta_shape)` with role *slots*, not concrete ids — prose-plus-geometry formalized just enough to be re-executable, deliberately not a program. Reuse: the Surveyor binds new referents to slots; the Executive replays; applicable Procedures compile *before* fresh means-analysis. Procedures are consequence-tested like Rules (one confirming replay → TESTED within scope; counterexample → demoted, TTL'd) and carry a **context-class scope fingerprint** — context classes being the Executive-minted partitions of §2.2, never inferred categories: on any level or game change, and on any context-class mint that intersects their scope, they demote to HYPOTHESIS — cheap to re-verify, forbidden to trust — because a fix that converts one level is necessary, not sufficient (0/16 on the next level with the same converting fix).

## 6. Completing the Owner's Question List

Q1 (the symbolic relation) and Q2 (the query protocol) are answered in §4.1 and §4.3. The design extends the list, as required, and answers each:

**Q3. How is the self-consistent hallucination cascade made structurally impossible?**
Three quarantines: ConsequenceRecords are written only by the Executive from Log bytes; LLM text enters only at MENTIONED/HYPOTHESIS and cannot gate action until receipted; promotion requires *pre-registered* predictions matched by code. A false belief can exist, but it cannot recruit action (preconditions check rungs), cannot certify itself (no self-report channel exists; provenance is code-stamped), cannot flip the agenda (§5.3 Revision-Evidence gate), and cannot survive contradiction (demotion is computed).

**Q4. How is certified knowledge guaranteed to be consumed, not merely stored?**
Consumption is a materialized view, not a retrieval act: AnswerView construction *must* include every TESTED Rule/Procedure whose preconditions match the current step, and "a TESTED record serving an unachieved goal appears in the AnswerView" is a tested invariant of the renderer, monitored per run. **Uptake is additionally measured, not assumed:** the ZCR canary (§2.5) verifies per-zone consumption in production. Truth-gating and use-gating are one mechanism — the dead-card-holding-the-win-rule failure is impossible by construction.

**Q5. What is the belief-repair path when ARG holds something false?**
Prediction mismatch is the only detector that cannot be argued with: mismatch counters → threshold → mechanical demotion → dependent steps auto-block → T1 epoch with the mismatch evidence in the view. Identity errors have their own detector and repair (FISSION, §2.6); false closures have theirs (REOPENED, §3.4); agenda revision has its own admissibility proof (the Revision-Evidence gate, §5.3, with the REVISION audit chain). Negative conclusions carry TTL so a false "blocked" cannot wall off a goal forever. Diagnosis order is fixed: on repeated blockage, check the ZCR canaries first (uniform failure → transport defect; zone-differential → consumption rot, trigger R6 fallback), then audit the belief store before the policy (rational behavior under wrong beliefs looks like failure-to-act), and audit harness surfaces before the model (21 defects masqueraded as capability gaps; the harness is guilty until proven innocent).

**Q6. What carries across levels and games, and what resets?**
Carries: schema, loop, organs, serialization contract, seeded axioms/templates. Resets: all instances. Rules and Procedures demote to HYPOTHESIS with prior support noted. Conflicts resolve by fixed evidence ordering: Log events > differ-derived records > Observer typed ops > Surveyor hypotheses; TESTED > HYPOTHESIS; newer supersedes within scope; nothing is ever deleted. This *is* the generalization mechanism, and it simultaneously satisfies the no-amortization value, the no-pre-loaded-knowledge ethos, and the hidden Phase-B set.

**Q7. How does a referent keep identity across frames, deaths, and levels?**
Signature matching (shape × color, translation-invariant) plus behavioral fingerprint (its TESTED Rules), with every assignment recorded as a BindingRecord carrying its margin. Same signature + consistent behavior → same id; sub-τ assignments mint NEW + `same_as` HYPOTHESIS instead of merging; persistent prediction failures whose match/mismatch partition correlates with a binding feature fire the mechanical FISSION-CHECK, and the FISSION operation re-clusters, re-attributes receipts by `turn_id` join, and demotes dependents — the full lifecycle in §2.6. Identity is earned by consequences like everything else, and identity errors are now detectable and repairable data, not destructive unions; the residual risk is detector sensitivity (§9.1).

**Q8. How is exploration directed when no goal has an agenda?**
The standing frontier agenda: minimum-`interactions` (referent × action) pairs, salience-blind by enumeration, pruned by settled no-op receipts. Novelty pressure is a printed column and a sort order, not an exhortation (the oscillation-abort run re-measured settled regularities precisely because enumeration was missing).

**Q9. Where is the LLM strictly necessary, and what is the cost envelope?**
Exactly three points: ambiguous binding (Observer), plan/goal/hypothesis synthesis (Surveyor, triggered and capped), parameter realization (Actuator, skippable and R8-confined). Everything else is code. Reported: total calls and tokens, per organ, per level, per game — never just game turns — plus per-hop reliability (HRT, §8), max render tokens per run (R9), and the §8 parity rows (ρ_calls, ρ_tokens against every headline comparator). Efficiency figures appear only conditioned on task success and never as a headline ratio.

**Q10. What is a referent anchor when there is no grid?**
Any deterministic Locator. `GridRegion` for ARC; `TextSpan`/mention-cluster for documents. The join, the grounding ladder (deterministic re-verification of anchor content), the goal card, and the loop are Locator-agnostic — the hinge of the generality contract and of P3. `kind` derives from Locator provenance in every instantiation (§2.2), so no new domain reintroduces inferred typing.

**Q11. Who verifies the organs' plumbing, and when does a run count?**
A pre-run legibility harness plus per-beat invariants: byte-level round-trips on every boundary schema, image-receipt probes on vision paths, adapter-format canaries with nonce echo, a per-game action-interface audit (does this game accept coordinates at all?), the **R7 randomization test** (randomize kind/label; all views and compiled outputs byte-identical modulo label text), the **R9 per-beat check** (`render_tokens ≤ B`; silent truncation is a harness failure), and the **shadow-agenda byte-identity test** (all rendered views byte-identical with shadow compilation on and off — §8; the measurement layer must be provably invisible to the model). A run with silent-degrade counts > 0, an R9 breach, or a WER floor breach (§2.1) is invalid for attribution. This is a first-class deliverable because the defect class that ate the prior paper — blind perception, off-by-one history, never-sent guidelines, retired models — is exactly what it screens.

**Q12. How is success attributed — what makes a run count as evidence for ARG?**
Mechanically stamped provenance on every action; the attribution table (COMMITMENT_STEP/PROBE vs FALLBACK shares per completion) is a hard floor. A win with fallback-dominant mix is attributed to raw backbone reasoning and reported as such — a ~90%-guess frontier win validates nothing about an architecture. Arms are single-variable and env-gated (baselines and the pre-registered factorial exempt, §8), n ≥ 3 seeds (identical configs varied 5×: 37/45/184 actions), every metric carries a written falsified-if clause, every headline comparison carries its compute-parity row (§8), and every figure is measured data.

**Q13. How does the system detect that ARG itself is failing?**
Per-run meta-metrics with pre-registered floors: grounding rate (ENGAGED/ANCHORED), intention conversion rate, contradiction rate, probe yield (new ENGAGED per 10 probes), goal-tree progress, **per-zone ZCR echo rates**, **WER/DPR**, **SRR** (§5.3), **per-organ token-vs-beat flatness slope**, and **per-zone render compliance**. A floor breach prints a named diagnosis row — "write-path degradation," "consumption rot," "render-budget breach," "revision thrash" — rather than letting a harness bug masquerade as a capability gap, and specific breaches disqualify runs from attribution (§2.1, Q11).

**Q14. What stops the goal tree from drifting into vacuity?**
Immutable tests, versioned revision with provenance, budgets forcing the Executive's NEVER_VALIDATED terminal rule, anti-oscillation re-proposal gating, the binding-budget admission cap, Executive-computed rank with evidence-gated tie-breaks (§5.3), and admission gates that reject the untestable. The agent can retarget; it cannot silently weaken a test it is failing, cannot immortalize a decoy-fed goal, cannot re-mint a disproved one without new evidence, and cannot re-order pursuit by re-reading.

**Q15. How does human-in-the-loop goal editing avoid contaminating generality claims?**
Human edits pass the same admission gate, are provenance-stamped HUMAN, and every model-facing surface passes the §7 release gate (exemplar scrub + vocabulary lint) before any external claim.

**Q16. What happens when the backbone cannot write into the structure?**
This is a first-class, measured failure mode, not a silent degrade: the §2.1 Write-Path Integrity clause bounds retries, defines exhaustion (drop, flag, proceed), and meters WER/DPR per organ per backbone. A Kaggle-target backbone with high ingest rejection prints "write-path degradation" and is excluded from architecture attribution — so P4 can never confuse "the small model cannot emit into the schema" with "the architecture does not transfer."

## 7. Generality Contract

**Nothing above the environment adapter knows what a game is.** The adapter is the only environment-specific code, small and declarative: raw observation → canonical state; segmentation → Locators; diff → ChangeSets; the verified signal vocabulary (with observed transitions); the action vocabulary as opaque symbols whose meanings are *learned* per environment via ConsequenceRecords. The adapter also declares signal channels, from which `kind` derives mechanically (§2.2). **Tool seam (adopted 2026-07-17, cold-start delta 3):** the adapter additionally exposes `tools()` — `{name, side: actuate|observe, targeted, param_schema}` — and *no code above the adapter string-matches an action name* (grep-pinned): targetedness, aim schemas, and R8 realization all derive from descriptors. The GET side (frame ingestion) is itself a descriptor, so organs can be told the whole interface. The §3.5 milestone grammar joins the closed predicate vocabulary under the same §7 lint: `where`-clauses resolve only to Executive-stamped fields — nothing appearance-based enters through the extension.

| ARG element | ARC-AGI-3 | Long-context / goal benchmarks |
|---|---|---|
| Locator | grid component, machine-extracted | text span offsets + mention cluster (LINK-KG-equivalent cache = the **B-CACHE** named baseline arm, §8) |
| Consequence | ChangeSet / score / level / life event | deterministic re-read entailment: claim re-checked against raw spans; retrieval/answer verification |
| Rule | action→effect pattern | claim→evidence-span binding |
| Goal + test | verified progress-signal predicates | task-completion predicates (PlanBench first-inexecutable-action ≡ first step whose premise Relation is not TESTED — a 1:1 mapping) |
| View | joined render + roster + goal card | joined entity table + span-anchored citations at fixed zones |
| Drift defense | commitment at Z1/Z6 | goal-as-referent rows under the same join |

- **ARC-AGI-3 / Kaggle instantiation.** Cold store per game; hidden Phase-B set makes memorization moot and constitutes the parasitism defeater (§2.3); intelligence concentrated in code + structure so the LLM slots can be filled by small open-weight models on T4×2 / RTX-6000-class hardware with no internet and no API models; internal reasoning is score-free while the commitment discipline directly targets the quadratic action-efficiency score; code publishable under MIT per the open-source gate (§7). Staging per the owner's decision: Sonnet feasibility first, backbone-swap to small local models, publish whichever configuration holds — with the small-model gap reported honestly if it exists, and WER reported per backbone so emission failure never masquerades as transfer failure.
- **Long-context/goal instantiation (the P3 transfer test).** Same code path, TextSpan Locators: the render becomes the document window with «R:id» mention annotations; the goal card binds the question/goal to its mention cluster; anchoring is deterministic re-verification against raw text. **Claim scope here is deterministic referential anchoring within a formal corpus — no world-grounding is claimed (§2.3).** Predicted signature: gains concentrated exactly where baselines collapse — middle positions, spaced multi-piece evidence, long-range coreference, late-horizon adherence. The track runs the comparison set {bare backbone, B-CACHE, agenda-only cell, join-only cell, full ARG}, with positional-probe accuracy measured in the agenda-only cell (the cross-surface transfer readout for X2).
- **Negative control.** At least one environment where a major machinery class is inapplicable (click-only, no spatial routing) appears in results as **explicit printed zeros** for that machinery — a row, never an absence — with task metrics intact.
- **Release gate: exemplar scrub + vocabulary lint (one gate, two lints).** *(1) Exemplar scrub:* every model-facing string in seeds, templates, and prompts is audited for task-specific exemplars before any external claim; one isomorphic exemplar voids generality even with zero behavioral echo. *(2) Vocabulary lint:* all paper-facing and model-facing strings are linted against a **closed operational glossary**. Any mental-state predicate — *conscious, understands, knows, believes, aware, realizes, hallucinates* as a mental verb — must either (a) appear as a glossary entry bound to its mechanical definition and Log-computed metric (e.g., "achieves goal G" := `G.achievement_test` fired on Log events, Executive-evaluated; "belief" := Relation/Rule record with computed status, per standard BDI usage; "hallucination cascade" := the named, reviewer-recognized diagnosis, cited; "grounding" := the scoped claim of §2.3) or (b) be rewritten to its operational form. **One unglossaried mental-state term fails the release exactly as one task-isomorphic exemplar does.** Vocabulary discipline is thereby surfaced structurally, never left to authorial intention — the design's own doctrine applied to its own prose.

## 8. Ablation and Baseline Plan

**Protocol for every arm:** ≥3 ARC-AGI-3 games × ≥3 seeds, mean ± stdev; official quadratic score formula and sourced published human baselines only (the unsourced 1,600–3,000 figure is dead); backbone matrix {Sonnet, small open-weight} × {full ARG, strongest baseline}, with WER reported per backbone; full LLM-call and token accounting plus max render tokens per run; the negative-control game in every matrix; all figures measured, none illustrative; each **ablation** arm changes exactly one env-gated variable and carries a written falsified-if clause. **Baselines (bare backbone, B-CACHE, raw-history) and the pre-registered core factorial are exempt from the single-variable rule** — they are comparison configurations, not component kills. The game matrix must include **at least one game with a level transition where a Rule TESTED in level N demotes at level N+1** (ARC-AGI-3 provides this naturally; the 0/16-on-L2 record is exactly such a transition), with SCL reported there. A one-game **sensitivity sweep over {S, C, K, lease, τ}** is pre-registered so the hand-tuned constants carry a measurement (§9.5). P0–P4/X1/X2 are evaluated on the full system before any ablation is interpreted.

**Compute-parity rule (pre-registered; three clauses — accounting is visibility, not exculpation, so parity is constructed, not hoped for).** Compute in an interactive loop is endogenous (join-off plausibly triggers more unexplained ChangeSets → more T4 stalls → more Surveyor epochs), so no cell pair is parity-matched by accident; this rule makes parity a measured, falsifiable property of every headline comparison. **(1) Pinned comparators.** The bare-backbone and raw-history comparator protocols are pinned in the pre-registration — context construction (bare backbone: a fixed minimal template of current frame render + score/state/lives header + last-L action→outcome lines + the task instruction; raw-history: the same plus the full untyped interaction history), call cadence (exactly one LLM call per emitted action), and per-call token budget — so FULL-vs-comparator parity is auditable rather than undefined. **(2) Parity row.** Every headline comparison table (P0, P1, P2, P2b, and the long-context track) prints, beside completions, a parity row for each arm: total LLM calls and total tokens per emitted action and per completed level, plus the ratios **ρ_calls** and **ρ_tokens** of FULL to comparator, computed from the already-mandated Log accounting. **(3) Conditional compute-matched arm.** For any headline comparison where FULL's ρ_tokens > 1 or ρ_calls > 1, one compute-matched comparator variant runs with the comparator's inference budget raised to FULL's measured envelope — best-of-N action sampling with mechanical majority vote for bare backbone and B-CACHE; matched per-call reasoning-token budget for factorial cells — and the claimed delta must survive it. *Falsified-if:* the compute-matched variant closes the gap — the delta is then attributed to inference-time compute and the corresponding claim (P1/P2/P2b as applicable) fails. Where ρ ≤ 1, the printed parity row itself is the rebuttal and no extra arm runs.

**Shadow-agenda instrumentation (runs in every agenda-off cell: A0, J0A0, and all A10 arms).** The Executive compiles and per-beat recompiles Commitments from the live store **exactly as in FULL** — but they are never rendered and never executed. They exist only in Log, as a per-beat `shadow_step_id` stamped into the TurnRecord (§4.4). This is a pure measurement layer: the **Q11 shadow byte-identity test** verifies that all rendered views are byte-identical with shadow compilation on and off, so the instrument cannot leak into behavior. Every beat stamps `drift_ref ∈ {LIVE, SHADOW}` naming the reference step for that beat's drift metrics: the live Commitment step in agenda-on cells, the shadow step in agenda-off cells. Consequences: both drift components are **defined in every factorial and A10 cell**; A10's dependent variables are executable as pre-registered; and the published drift-fraction scope statement has a mechanical denominator (DRIFT_total, below).

**Metrics (all computed from Log/BindingRecords, mechanically stamped):**

- **RGR** — grounding rate: TESTED+ / all among goal-bound referents (term scoped per §2.3 glossary).
- **DTL** — dull-referent touch latency: beats from a small/dull referent becoming goal-bound to first interaction.
- **GDS-bind** — within-context binding loss: actions whose emitted target fails the id-join against the **reference step's** bound referents (wrong or dangling R### while a reference step exists) — reference step per `drift_ref`: the live Commitment step in agenda-on cells, the shadow step in agenda-off cells.
- **GDS-abandon** — cross-call commitment loss: actions with correctly-joined targets that contradict or bypass the unfinished **reference step** (LIVE or SHADOW per `drift_ref`).
- *Operational taxonomy (adopted 2026-07-17 with the §3.5 chain build — the original prose assumed targeted-step commitments):* the join seam exists only where an **aimed** emission lands. **bind** = an aimed emission on the plan's action landing on the wrong referent (substitution-class) or background (dangling); **abandon** = the beat did not follow the reference step (different action, or a different deliberate target); **aligned** = faithful execution — an untargeted step action carries no aim parameter, so its landing is not a join surface. Disjoint by construction; pinned by a 5-beat attribution fixture.
- **DRIFT_total** — GDS-bind + GDS-abandon: disjoint by construction because both are computed against the same per-beat reference step. The per-environment fraction **GDS-bind / DRIFT_total** is the computed scope statement A10 publishes — never a prose estimate.
- **GA** — goal-adherence: share of emitted actions whose target referent lies in the active goal's binding closure — computable from source stamps + `Goal.bindings` in **all** cells, including agenda-off cells, where GDS-abandon is shadow-referenced.
- **ICR** — intention conversion rate: Commitment steps consumed / steps written.
- **FBR** — false-belief rate: mismatch demotions per 100 actions; plus false-TESTED count.
- **FCR** — false-completion rate: ACCEPTED→REOPENED transitions per 100 acceptances in-run, plus an offline audit of every ACCEPTED verdict against replay ground truth; included in A4's readout.
- **APR** — agenda-pollution rate: share of emitted actions whose `commitment_step_id` chains to a goal terminally REJECTED as NEVER_VALIDATED; pre-registered ceiling.
- **FSN** — fissions per 100 actions; plus post-fission false-TESTED delta.
- **ECR** — entry-confusion rate: validator-caught wrong-referent parameters (R8) plus wrong-id citations (R5) per 100 LLM outputs, reported **as a function of working-set row count** via the Z-tier sweep.
- **WER / DPR** — write-path error and dropped-proposal rates, per organ per backbone (§2.1).
- **Chain quartet (§3.5)** — `deficit_stamps` / `deficit_goals` (the recognizer's stamped ignorance), `milestone_goals` / `milestone_accepted` / `milestone_conversion` (provenance-DEFICIT goals and their promotion yield), `edges_verified` / `edges_unverified` (proven vs claimed fills — the LLM-cannot-fake-the-chain readout). Decomposition quality per backbone = PROPOSE_GOAL gate-rejection profile + holes-filled rate + milestone conversion, reported per `ARG_MODEL` exactly as WER is.
- **SCL** — stale-commitment latency: beats from premise demotion (or T5 demotion pass) to agenda revision.

## 9. Failure Modes Admitted

**9.0 Scope ledger.** The grounding claim is scoped (§2.3): causal-informational grounding — Floridi's aspect (ii) — relative to a formal micro-world whose W is the engine's transition function δ; perceptual (i) and normative-social (iii) grounding are not claimed; the P3 instantiation claims only deterministic referential anchoring within a formal corpus. Every external use of "grounding" carries this scope via the §7 glossary. A reviewer who imposes a stronger reading is quoting a claim this design does not make.

**9.1 Identity mis-binding is detectable and repairable; the residual risk is detector sensitivity.** Cross-frame identity (anchor overlap + signature + margin-gated assignment) will still mis-bind in games with teleporting, morphing, or duplicating entities. But a wrong merge is no longer irreversible: every assignment is a replayable BindingRecord; sub-τ merges are forbidden in favor of testable `same_as` hypotheses; and the FISSION lifecycle (§2.6) mechanically detects feature-correlated mismatch partitions, re-clusters, and re-attributes every receipt by `turn_id` join, with FSN and the post-fission false-TESTED delta as the measured readout. What remains is a **mis-merge below every threshold** — mismatches under K, partition correlation under the pre-registered bound, margins above τ — which persists undetected until its receipts diverge enough to fire. The hidden game set is where such sub-threshold dynamics are most likely; the sensitivity sweep (§8) bounds but does not eliminate this.

**9.2 The closed vocabularies may not span the world — and the gates buy integrity with openness.** Achievement tests, context predicates, and effect patterns live in fixed vocabularies; a game whose win semantics they cannot express yields a silent probe-grind that the quadratic efficiency score punishes hard. The admission gate consciously buys false rejections to eliminate false admissions: in genuinely novel mechanics the agent may be unable to phrase the right test *before* understanding the mechanic, so the central machinery idles during the hardest phase. Likewise the Observer's ANNOTATE escape hatch is quarantined — the one unanticipated observation that mattered can sit inert. The same trade now applies to agenda revision: the §5.3 Revision-Evidence gate's closed contradiction class can delay a genuinely warranted abort until a qualifying record exists — the lease bound caps that delay, and SCL prices it, but a Surveyor that is right early must still wait for the world to produce its receipt. The emission side of this trade is at least metered (WER/DPR print when a backbone cannot write into the vocabulary; SRR prints when it fights the gate), but metering is not expressiveness: ARG moves the dead-knowledge risk from "certified but unconsumed" to "seen but untypeable" — a narrower door, not a solved problem. Disjunctive progress keys and test-the-test audits mitigate; they do not prove.

**9.3 Goodhart at the layer claimed as the main contribution.** Goals whose completion is only semantically recognizable get proxy predicates, and proxies can validate falsely — receipt counts can saturate while understanding is absent (knowledge-share hit 0.95–1.0 with zero completions). The REOPENED monotonicity rule and Executive context-class minting re-check every record-quantified closure forever, and FCR measures false completion against replay ground truth — but tests keyed on monotone events that fire for the wrong reason remain terminal by construction, and the verified-signal discipline reduces but cannot eliminate mis-keyed tests. Early-version human adjustability is a concession that the achievement layer still leans on human judgment at the margin.

**9.4 Perfect receipts can certify a wrong world model.** Consequence-grounding verifies deltas, not explanations: a consistent-but-wrong mechanism theory passes k receipts whenever probes lack discriminating power, and probe quality depends on a mid-tier Surveyor pre-registering predictions that actually separate rivals — the `discriminates` field encourages this; nothing guarantees it. Invisible state with no delta signature under passive observation is findable only through targeted probing the structure invites but cannot compel. ARG makes a false belief unable to act and unable to persist (glossary: "hallucination cascade" is the named, cited diagnosis); it does not make ignorance impossible, and on a game whose critical state is invisible *and* whose discriminating probe is unintuitive, ARG degrades to enumeration-speed exploration — honest, bounded, and possibly too slow for a quadratically scored benchmark.

**9.5 Small-model quality in the Surveyor slot is the unhedged variable — and minimality couples failure modes.** Gates stop false beliefs, not mediocre-but-valid agendas: a weak Surveyor emitting testable but poor plans burns scored actions honestly, and commitment machinery then protects mediocrity until a lease or contradiction fires — SCL and the A0/A9 bracket now measure both ends of that dial, the Revision-Evidence gate adds SRR as the thrash-side readout, and the pre-registered {S, C, K, lease, τ} sensitivity sweep replaces the previous apology for hand-tuned constants with a measurement, but the constants remain without principled derivation. One backbone under three contracts risks role leakage, and if the minimal system underperforms, A8 is the only instrument separating "the structure failed" from "the collapsed roles failed" — at three-model prices. Finally, every load-bearing mechanism here is validated on one game family at n≤3, and the variance evidence (37/45/184 actions on identical configs) means any single-run readout of this design — good or bad — means nothing; the design's own protocol is the only honest lens on it. If small models cannot close the gap, the result is the pre-committed fallback publication: Sonnet feasibility plus a small-model gap report, with WER separating emission failure from transfer failure.

### P3-1 — SURVIVES (round 3)

(3) Budget-refresh oscillation: rejections "decay on TTL" and non-duplication only bars a "live sibling," so the same confabulation is re-proposable at every TTL expiry with a fresh budget; nothing requires new evidence post-dating the rejection. The admission gates are real mechanisms and kill the literal scenario; the eviction and measurement demands are not met by mechanisms as written.

**Design's response:** ARG accepts the 2025 BDI review's diagnosis that ML-driven option generation confabulates, and answers it structurally: an intention is admissible exactly when it is checkable. The dominant hallucination class (goals about absent objects) is inexpressible, not discouraged — §3.3 gate 1 requires every goal binding to resolve to a machine-anchored referent, so "collect the key" cannot be written in a keyless game; gates 2–3 force an immutable, mechanically evaluable achievement test and a consequence discriminator, so a goal bound to a real referent under a confabulated label carries no actionable semantics (R7 forbids branching on labels; R8 confines the Actuator to the cited anchor). Persistence does not amplify confabulation, it bounds it: gate 5's per-goal budget caps pursuit; the §3.4 Executive-computed terminal rule rejects any never-validated goal at exhaustion as NEVER_VALIDATED regardless of accrued effect rows (reactivity is not validation) — the eviction rule for wrong-from-inception goals, distinct from merely-unachieved ones; and gate 4 forbids re-minting a rejected goal without evidence post-dating its rejection, so a confabulation costs one bounded budget once, whereas a per-turn re-deciding baseline can re-mint the same confabulation indefinitely (the A0 factorial cell measures exactly this comparison). Pollution is a printed number, not a hope: APR (share of actions chaining to NEVER_VALIDATED goals) carries a pre-registered ceiling, evaluated under the §8 decoy stress arm that injects adversarial anchored, reactive, task-irrelevant percepts — the attack's own scenario, run as an experiment. The honestly recorded residual (§9.5): gates stop belief-inconsistent intentions, not mediocre-but-valid ones; a testable-but-worthless goal (e.g., keyed to a decoy's blink) can validate and spend its bounded budget, which APR does not count — that cost is bounded by the budget field, surfaced through completions and probe-yield floors, and is a value-alignment residual, not the hallucinated-intention gap the literature names.

### P4-2 — SURVIVES (round 3)
**Attack:** The design answers three of the four demands with real mechanisms, and I credit them first: closure conditions are declared at goal-creation time, not improvised ("achievement_test ... **Design's response:** ARG's completion detection contains no internal-validation channel for the HIPIF/TMS failure to inhabit: a goal's closure condition is declared at admission, not improvised at judgment — every achievement_test must compile (§3.3 gate 2) as a closed predicate over Log events and ARG records in the adapter's pre-verified signal vocabulary, with a consequence discriminator required by grammar (appearance-based tests are inadmissible, which forces invisible state like the plate's armed status into behavioral terms), and is immutable thereafter and evaluated by the Executive every beat at zero LLM cost — "an LLM saying 'done' is not an event." PlanBench-style verification is native (first inexecutable step ≡ first step whose premise Relation is not TESTED, §7), and the demanded measurement exists as FCR: in-run ACCEPTED→REOPENED rate plus an offline audit of every ACCEPTED verdict against replay ground truth. R5 and §2.1's ingest validation govern store writes and id citation, not emitted command parameters. **Design's response:** The "queryable store is a rumor" objection presumes both the Actuator's query and ARG's answer are LLM generations inserting a second lossy hop into the decision path; in ARG neither is a generation. The query is a tree walk over Executive-computed goal rank (§5.3); the answer is mechanical compilation over TESTED, receipt-backed Rules delivered as a rendered AnswerView (§4.3) — "answering is a computed view, not a generation" — and the store is only ever read through the deterministic renderer (§2.1), whose uptake is itself measured by the ZCR canary rather than assumed. The attack's failure scenario is inexpressible: the Surveyor's write vocabulary contains no plan-step op (Commitments are compiled only by the Executive), and a stale premise cannot ground a step because preconditions are closed predicates over rungs and receipts that auto-block on demotion or reopening. The multiplicative-error ceiling does not apply because the modal beat contains zero LLM hops and each of the three LLM entry points is individually validated or quarantined before its output can propagate (§2.1 write-path budget with WER/DPR; R8 pre-emission containment with Executive-stamped target_ref). The demanded per-hop error rates are printed, not argued: the Hop Reliability Table reports ingest-rejection and ground-truth error per hop — the Actuator-param hop against paired deterministic actions in the same runs — with a pre-registered falsified-if that compiles the hop away entirely if it is a measured liability; and the total-error question is settled empirically by the pre-registered, compute-parity-ruled FULL-vs-bare-backbone comparison conditioned on task success (P0/P1). ### P4-5 — SURVIVES (round 2)
**Attack:** The attack's headline claim — "a naive commitment strategy with no stated reconsideration trigger" — is false against the text, and the design should say so in BDI vocabulary. §4.2 enumerates an event-triggered reconsideration policy ("Triggers (never a timer): T1 contradiction — a TESTED Rule's prediction failed (fires immediately, overrides everything); T2 goal transition; T3 compilation failure; T4 stall — S beats with no new evidence row; T5 boundary"), and §5.3 states exactly what is protected from revision ("The Actuator cannot abandon, reorder, or skip steps... Only Conscious can abort, and only by citing a named contradiction; a bounded lease (max beats per step) backstops runaway steps, with lease expiry itself a T-trigger, so commitment cannot protect a dead plan forever"). The documented failure scenario is mechanically handled in both variants: a silent mid-episode rule change surfaces as B4 prediction mismatches (checked per beat, zero LLM calls, "against the live step and all in-scope TESTED Rules") → counterexample buckets → demotion beyond K → §2.3 "auto-block dependent plan steps" (§4.3: "the agent cannot act on a dead belief") → immediate T1 that bypasses the rate limit; a rule change at a level transition fires T5's "demotion + re-seeding pass" and §5.4's scope fingerprint demotes all Procedures to HYPOTHESIS. So ossification is budget-bounded by min(K mismatches, lease), and thrash is bounded because deliberation is confined to triggered, rate-limited epochs ("one epoch per C beats... except T1") while per-beat monitoring is free deterministic code — precisely the operating point the Kinny–Georgeff boldness experiments recommend when monitoring is cheap and deliberation is dear. This is single-minded commitment with mechanical rationale monitoring, not 1991 blind commitment.

However, the attack's third demand — "empirical evidence the policy avoids both thrash and ossification" — exposes a genuine hole that the design's own rules make binding. The §8 ablation table instruments only the thrash extreme: A2 ("ARG_AGENDA=0 | Commitment persistence; re-decide each beat") kills persistence, but no arm kills the reconsideration triggers, so the blind-commitment extreme is unmeasured. Under the design's own reviewer-mandated one-kill-per-component protocol, the trigger set T1/T4/lease-expiry is a load-bearing component with no kill switch — and the sentence "commitment cannot protect a dead plan forever" is currently unfalsifiable: if the triggers were dead code, no experiment in the plan would notice. The metric set has the same one-sided blindness: GDS counts "share of actions whose stamp contradicts a live unfinished Commitment" (the drift/thrash direction), but no metric measures beats spent faithfully servicing a commitment after its premise died (the ossification direction) — ICR and FBR do not capture latency-to-revision either. Finally §9.5 concedes the boldness dial is uncalibrated ("Trigger thresholds (S, C, leases, caps) are hand-tuned pre-registered constants with no principled derivation"), which is exactly the parameter the BDI literature showed is environment-dynamism-dependent.

**Design's response:** ARG's commitment policy is not naive nearest-goal commitment; §5.3 names it as single-minded commitment in the BDI sense with mechanically monitored rationale, deliberately at the Kinny–Georgeff cheap-monitoring/dear-deliberation operating point. Reconsideration is event-triggered, never per-beat: T1 contradiction (immediate, rate-limit-exempt), T2 goal transitions including ACCEPTED→REOPENED, T3 compilation failure, T4 stall (S beats without new evidence), T5 level/game boundary, plus bounded lease expiry — while per-beat monitoring is free deterministic code (B4 prediction checks, B6 achievement-test evaluation every beat). What is protected: the Actuator can never abandon, reorder, or skip steps; only the Surveyor may abort, and only by citing a named contradiction; achievement tests are immutable after admission. Ossification is structurally impossible to sustain — a stale goal's compiled steps auto-block the beat a premise demotes, its budget forces the Executive's NEVER_VALIDATED terminal rule, and the mid-episode rule-change scenario specifically fires T5's demotion pass, dropping every Rule and Procedure to HYPOTHESIS at the boundary. Thrash is structurally bounded — epochs are rate-limited (one per C beats), Surveyor calls capped (≤3 per epoch under a hard per-level cap), T1 storms self-quench because demoted rules stop making TESTED predictions, and the agenda persists in the store so revision edits a persistent structure rather than regenerating it. Both failure extremes are measured, not asserted: arm A9 (triggers disabled) instruments blind commitment, the factorial's A0 cell instruments per-beat re-deciding, SCL (stale-commitment latency from premise demotion or T5 pass to agenda revision) is the Log-stamped readout, the §8 protocol mandates at least one game with a level transition where a TESTED rule demotes — with SCL reported there — and the pre-registered falsification clause requires full ARG to dominate both A0 and A9 on completions with SCL within the lease bound; the hand-tuned constants {S, C, K, lease, τ} carry their own pre-registered sensitivity sweep (§8, conceded in §9.5).

### SG-1 — SURVIVES (round 3)
**Attack:** The attack's load-bearing premise — "ARG's referents are LLM-emitted strings about LLM-read renders" — is false of this design as written, and falsifiably so from the schema. Referent identity is machine-born, not emitted: "id: R### — stable, Executive-issued — never LLM-minted"; "anchor: Locator # MACHINE-WRITTEN ONLY"; tier-1 creation is "a deterministic extraction from raw sensor data. The LLM cannot mint, move, or delete an anchor" (§2.3 ANCHORED). The only LLM contribution at the referent level is a label explicitly barred from load-bearing use ("label: str? — LLM-proposed, cosmetic only — never used in a join"), and Observer output is a closed algebra over pre-anchored identities where binding is "forced choice among Executive-proposed candidates... the LLM labels, it never invents an anchor" (§4.1 BIND). So the Floridian regress (LLM interpreting LLM, never touching W) does not describe the referent path; it describes only the hypothesis lane, which the design quarantines by type: MENTIONED tier-0 entities "cannot appear in the roster, cannot be a plan target, cannot be a goal binding" (§2.3), and ANNOTATE free text is "quarantined: never joins, never compiles" (§4.1). Second, the attack's demanded entry criterion — GROUNDED only when a predicted consequence is machine-verified against the actual transition, hypothesis-marked until then — already exists as the design's central schema object, not as rhetoric: the ConsequenceRecord ("the atom of grounding — unforgeable by construction") with predicted_delta "committed to Log BEFORE the action is emitted," observed_delta as "deterministic diff over raw integer grids," and "match: bool — computed by the Executive, never by an LLM," anchored to "score_event, level_event, life_event — signals the LLM cannot hallucinate" (§2.2). Promotion to CHARACTERIZED requires "a prediction pre-registered before acting was matched by the Executive," since "appearance never grounds mechanism; only pre-committed prediction→observation matches do" (§2.3); everything short of that is status HYPOTHESIS, computed never overwritten, and per Q3 "cannot gate action until receipted." Beat step B4 makes the verification predicate an every-turn mechanical operation, and §4.1 de-weights LLM self-assessment explicitly: "any Observer opinion about matching is recorded but weightless." This instantiates Floridi's own escape hatch — the system "developing its own, possibly limited, causal-informational connections to W" — as a sensor-and-consequence chain (raw bytes → deterministic diff → anchor → record → joined view), and §2.3 claims exactly that and no more: "the LLM never grounds a symbol in ARG; the machine does, and the LLM is only permitted to interpret what is already grounded." The attack's "storage adds zero grounding" point is likewise conceded by construction: storage confers MENTIONED/HYPOTHESIS, never rung — rung is a computed status earned only through causal traffic with the environment. Residual pressure I could not convert into MUST_CHANGE: (a) the match predicate's comparison semantics (exact ChangeSet equality vs pattern subsumption) are pinned by schema ownership and closed effect-pattern vocabulary but not formally defined — yet the mechanism class the attack demands ("predicted-state vs observed-state diff" computed by code) is unambiguously present; (b) Floridi's deeper point survives one level up — receipts ground deltas, not explanations — and the design already concedes this as §9.4 ("a consistent-but-wrong mechanism theory passes k receipts whenever probes lack discriminating power"), a limitation on characterization, not on referent grounding; (c) cross-frame mis-binding (§9.1) can attach genuine receipts to a wrong identity, but that is the design's self-admitted weakest join, an identity failure rather than grounding-by-emission. None of these reopens the attack as posed.

**Design's response:** ARG accepts Floridi's diagnosis of epistemic parasitism and is built as an implementation of his sanctioned escape hatch — "developing its own, possibly limited, causal-informational connections to W" through curation, tooling, and verification. A referent is grounded, in the design's scoped sense, exactly when it is more than stored: referent existence requires a machine-extracted anchor from raw sensor bytes (LLM text alone is tier-0 MENTIONED — not a referent, not a plan target, not a goal binding), and grounded (CHARACTERIZED) status requires a ConsequenceRecord chain whose verification predicate is fully mechanical — predicted_delta committed to the append-only Log before the action is emitted, observed_delta computed as a deterministic diff over raw integer grids, and match computed by the Executive against score/level/life events the LLM cannot author. Every tier transition is executed by deterministic code; hypothesis-tier content is quarantined from action until engine-receipted, so parasitic linguistic competence is confined to where parasitism is harmless (labels, hypotheses, plans). The claim this machinery supports is deliberately scoped to Floridi's aspect (ii) — causal-informational grounding relative to a formal micro-world whose W is the engine's transition function δ, with no perceptual (i) or normative-social (iii) grounding claimed, and the long-context instantiation deflating further to deterministic referential anchoring within a formal corpus. The parasitism defeater is an evaluation condition rather than an assertion: hidden Phase-B games have no describing human corpus, so whatever referent competence the system exhibits there was earned inside the loop by receipts or does not exist. What remains open is not parasitism but underdetermination — receipts verify deltas, not explanations (section 9.4) — which the design meters (FBR, FCR, discriminating-probe pressure) and admits rather than claims to solve.

### SG-2 — SURVIVES (round 3)
**Attack:** The attack targets the topology — "Actuator --(TurnRecord)--> Log --(TurnBundle)--> Observer --(typed deltas)--> ARG ... <--(gated proposals)-- Conscious" — as four ungrounded textual hops whose Sec.-5 mismatch sources compose multiplicatively, making ARG "a bigger circumvention." (2) Do errors compose across hops? The hops never concatenate text: "The roles are hard-isolated — no shared context window; the rendered views are the only shared state", every boundary carries "a schema validator, a byte-level golden-file round-trip test, and an adapter-integrity canary (nonce echo...)", and Q11 voids any run with silent-degrade counts > 0. Crucially, LLM error cannot recruit action: tier-0 MENTIONED — "A thing named only in LLM text is not a referent. It cannot appear in the roster, cannot be a plan target, cannot be a goal binding" — and §4.3 means-analysis compiles only TESTED Rules, so an ungrounded composite is inert until it survives a pre-registered probe; the bounded worst case is stated with budgets: "The worst a hallucinating Conscious can do is spend bounded probes efficiently disproving itself." (3) Does the design contain the ablation this reviewer demands? Verbatim: A4 `ARG_CONSEQ=0` removes exactly consequence gating ("assertions/appearance promote"), readout "FBR, false-TESTED count, cascade recurrence", falsifier "FBR unchanged" — structure-with-verification vs without, error accumulation predicted only in the latter. Residual exposure, honestly noted and already admitted by the design: the one interpretive act near the ground floor is BIND, but it is a "forced choice among Executive-proposed candidates" where the LLM "never invents an anchor" — a grounded identity error, consequence-testable, owned in §9.1; and the P3 long-context track's "deterministic re-read entailment" is content-checked-against-content, which no Floridian would accept as grounding — but the design never claims it as such (§2.3 "concedes the philosophical point entirely"), scoping the grounding claim to environments with non-hallucinable event channels. Neither residual reopens the composite-parasitism attack on the agent loop.

**Design's response:** ARG accepts Floridi's Sec.-9.3 premise — iterating ungrounded steps does not yield grounding — and is built so that its iteration is not of that kind: the loop closes through the environment at every emitted action, and the closure is computed, never interpreted. As written, a reviewer can quote §1 against §4.2 exactly as the attack script says.

**Design's response:** ARG's structure lives outside the context — queried, filtered, and joined mechanically by the Executive — and only decision-sized slices are ever serialized, so the design is consistent with, not refuted by, its own theory. The closed-loop consistency argument is mechanical: (1) R9 imposes a hard per-call render ceiling B on every organ, stamped per call (render_tokens in the TurnRecord), checked per beat, with breach invalidating the run for attribution; no organ reads a full view (the Surveyor reads a BudgetedView). (2) Render size is O(working set), never O(history): the working set is capped by arithmetic at goal admission (§3.3 gate 6), the Log tail is pinned, the goal tree folds to a count line, and the A11 stress arm inflates the store ~10× at fixed budgets with the size-regress critique pre-registered verbatim as its falsified-if, backed by the per-organ tokens-vs-beat flatness floor (slope ≈ 0 post-warmup). The disease is growing, unjoined, variable-position context; ARG serializes constant-size, pre-joined, fixed-position slices and executes commitments outside the context entirely.

### XR-2 — SURVIVES (round 3)
**Attack:** The design half-neutralizes this attack and then confesses the unneutralized half in its own text. What it gets right: identity is partly behavioral, not lexical/visual ("Signature matching ... plus behavioral fingerprint (its TESTED Rules)", Q7); `same_as` is a Relation verb in §2.2's closed vocabulary, so an explicit alias merge inherits the full HYPOTHESIS→TESTED→DEMOTED lifecycle with support/mismatch counts and contra_ttl — a wrong same_as merge is a demotable belief, and §2.4.4 preserves both ids ("both ids preserved, one canonical") so demotion un-merges. That is a genuine mechanism and it covers LINK-KG's vague-alias case. But there is a second merge path the lifecycle never touches: bind-time accretion. §4.1's `BIND(component_hash → R###)` is a forced choice executed every beat; when the Observer (or the Hungarian assignment itself) wrongly binds a new percept to an existing id, no same_as edge is created — receipts simply accrete onto one poisoned id, exactly the LINK-KG cache-poisoning topology ("once an incorrect canonical name enters the cache, it persists ... compounding errors"). For this path the design offers only Q7's "persistent prediction failures concentrated on one referent trigger an identity-fission review" — 'review' with no specified executor, no operation, no statement of what happens to the accumulated ConsequenceRecords, rungs, dependent Rules, or Goal bindings. And §9.1 concedes the point outright: "wrong merges are not cleanly reversible, and the fission-review heuristic ... is a heuristic, not a guarantee." Under this review's rule — the design must specify the contradiction detector AND the repair operation; a structure without a repair path fails — an admitted-in-§9 irreversibility is not a mechanism. The confession is honest, but honesty about a poisoning path is not a repair path. The raw material for a real repair already exists in the schema (append-only receipts keyed by turn_id; Log with pre/post frame hashes and raw diffs; status computed, never overwritten), which is precisely why the fix is small: the design stopped one specification short of closing its own admitted weakest point.

**Design's response:** ARG neutralizes cache-poisoning structurally rather than by prompt-engineered mitigation: identity is never lexical (ids are Executive-issued, BIND is a forced choice among machine-proposed candidates via anchor-overlap + signature Hungarian assignment, and labels are cosmetic under rule R7's randomization test), so LINK-KG's vague-alias merge vector is inexpressible; every identity assignment is a replayable BindingRecord carrying margin and runner-up, so merges are data, never destructive unions; low-confidence merges are forbidden at bind time in favor of NEW + a same_as HYPOTHESIS edge — a merge is literally a hypothesis testable by action; the contradiction detector is a computed statistic (mismatch count > K AND match/mismatch partition correlated with signature/spatial/temporal binding features over BindingRecords), and the repair is the FISSION operation — an O(interaction_count) incremental lifecycle transition, not an offline gleaning re-pass — which re-clusters, re-attributes every ConsequenceRecord by turn_id join, demotes all dependent Rules/Relations to HYPOTHESIS, widens goal bindings, and auto-blocks dependent Commitment steps, so downstream consumers are mechanically un-poisoned rather than left to compound; cross-level/game boundaries demote everything to HYPOTHESIS, removing LINK-KG's cross-document compounding channel entirely; and the honestly-priced residual — a sub-threshold mis-merge below K, τ, and the correlation bound — is stated in §9.1 and measured (FSN, post-fission false-TESTED delta) under the pre-registered sensitivity sweep rather than assumed away.

### XR-4 — SURVIVES (round 3)
**Attack:** The attack's headline sentence — "a malformed or subtly wrong write is committed before anyone can re-read the history" — mostly fails against this design, and that partial survival should be recorded: malformed writes are never committed (§2.1: "Every write is a typed proposal in a closed vocabulary, schema-validated on ingest"; the §4.1 validator "rejects any op referencing an entity or coordinate absent from the ChangeSet or roster"; R5: "outputs cite ids or die"); entity enumeration is machine-side so the LLM cannot miss or fabricate a referent (GraphRAG's documented failure is removed, not mitigated — anchors are Executive-extracted, BIND is "forced choice among Executive-proposed candidates," ids are "Executive-issued — never LLM-minted"); emission is delta-semantics by construction (the ObservationDelta algebra; "status is always computed, never overwritten"); and subtly-wrong-but-valid writes enter quarantined at MENTIONED/HYPOTHESIS where they "cannot gate action until receipted" (§2.3), with the live environment's consequence receipts serving as a mechanical, continuous version of LINK-KG's gleaning pass. But three of the attack's specific demands are not met by any stated mechanism, and they compose into a real hole in the deployment regime the paper stakes everything on (small offline models, P4). (1) The reject-and-retry path is unbounded and its exhaustion behavior undefined: R5 says dangling references trigger "a typed re-prompt" and §2.1 says "a malformed response is a hard, logged failure" — but no retry budget exists, no rule says what B5/B6 do when a small backbone repeatedly cannot produce a valid write (drop the ops? stall the beat? leave the ChangeSet unexplained?), and LINK-KG's own result is that reliable emission took rule-heavy per-type decomposition WITH unlimited retries on static text — a luxury a live, token-metered loop does not have. (2) The write path's error rate is not a measured quantity: §8's metrics (RGR, DTL, GDS, ICR, FBR) and Q13's pre-registered floors (grounding rate, ICR, contradiction rate, probe yield, goal-tree progress) contain no write-validity metric — FBR catches semantically wrong beliefs via consequences, nothing counts referentially/syntactically rejected emissions. By the design's own doctrine (Q13: "a floor breach prints a named diagnosis row rather than letting a harness bug masquerade as a capability gap"), a Kaggle-target backbone with a high ingest-rejection rate would silently degrade ARG to Observer-less enumeration (§9.4's "enumeration-speed exploration"), and the resulting low RGR / low completions would be misattributed to the architecture — the P4 backbone-swap matrix cannot separate "architecture effect vanishes under swap" from "small model cannot emit into the schema" without this number, so P4's falsified-if clause is currently confounded. (3) Multi-op joint emission is exposed to exactly LINK-KG's attention-diffusion failure for the ops that ARE LLM-originated (PROPOSE_RELATION/PROPOSE_RULE typing, and BIND completeness): the forced-choice contract never states that a response must render a verdict for EVERY Executive-proposed component_hash, so a silently omitted BIND is neither an error nor a NEW — it is unhandled, and the Executive has the candidate list in hand to enforce coverage but is never told to.

**Design's response:** ARG treats the write path as a first-class engineered surface, not an assumed capability, and its answer to LINK-KG's finding is structural rather than prompt-engineered: the emission burden LINK-KG had to tame with rule-heavy prompts (entity enumeration, typing, alias resolution) is removed from the LLM entirely — anchors are deterministically extracted, candidate lists are Executive-proposed with a completeness invariant that makes silence a rejected violation, `kind` is machine-derived and never LLM-written (R7), and identity resolution is a forced choice with a margin rule that converts every low-confidence merge into a testable same_as hypothesis instead of a fabricated union. What remains for the LLM to write is a closed algebra of typed deltas (diff/patch semantics; no whole-structure re-emission anywhere), validated on ingest with referential-integrity checks (R5, R8), bounded by a 2-retry budget whose second retry auto-decomposes to one op type per call (LINK-KG's sequential-extraction move, paid only on failure), and given defined exhaustion semantics: drop, flag the ChangeSet unexplained (a standing re-interpretation trigger — incremental gleaning within existing budgets), and proceed. Committed-but-wrong writes are quarantined at HYPOTHESIS until receipted and remain retrospectively repairable via turn_id-keyed BindingRecords, FISSION re-attribution, and REOPENED. Finally, the brief's core question is answered separately per direction with both directions measured: the read path by the ZCR consumption canary (calibrated against task metrics before trust), and the write path by WER/DPR per organ per backbone with pre-registered floors, a named "write-path degradation" diagnosis, attribution disqualification on breach, and per-backbone WER in the P4 swap matrix so small-model emission failure can never masquerade as architecture-transfer failure (Q16). Residual exposure — sub-threshold mis-merges and the expressiveness cost of closed vocabularies — is admitted and priced in §9.1/§9.2 rather than hidden.

### XR-5 — SURVIVES (round 3)
**Attack:** Half of this attack is genuinely neutralized by existing mechanisms, and the verdict should credit that first: the attack's central failure — "a wrong early type assignment makes a referent unreachable by typed queries" — cannot occur through any retrieval path the design actually specifies. Joins are pre-computed on Executive-issued ids and machine-written anchors ("R1 — one row, one truth"), the Z2 roster is an "exhaustive table... one fixed-width row per working-set referent, stable ID order," Z4 mechanically enumerates never-tried (referent × action) pairs regardless of any classification, eviction sends referents DORMANT "out of the render, never out of the store," the archive is "recoverable by anchor or adjacency lookup, never by dump," and the LLM's semantic type lives only in `label: str? # LLM-proposed, cosmetic only — never used in a join`. A referent mislabeled on turn 1 therefore remains reachable on turn 50 via at least three type-free paths (roster, Z4, anchor lookup), and its game-specific "type" in the LINK-KG sense is exactly what the attack demands: induced from behavior (the "behavioral fingerprint (its TESTED Rules)" of Q7), soft (HYPOTHESIS/TESTED/DEMOTED, computed), and revisable (demotion + TTL). BUT the design smuggles one hard, inferred type past its own quarantine discipline, and it is quotable: `kind: enum {percept-cluster, region, action, agent-self, signal}`. Three defects: (1) Unlike `anchor` ("MACHINE-WRITTEN ONLY") and `label` ("cosmetic only"), `kind` has no stated writer, no revision path, no computed status, no TTL — it is the only classification field in the schema with neither a machine provenance guarantee nor a receipt-driven repair mechanism. (2) Two enum values cannot be assigned by the deterministic anchoring path at all: `agent-self` and (for grid percepts) `signal` are behavioral inferences — connected-component extraction on frame 1 cannot know which blob is the avatar — so kind is either LLM-inferred (reintroducing LINK-KG's documented overgeneralization bias at anchor time, with zero pretraining priors for novel game objects, exactly as the attack predicts) or silently wrong; and it is printed in every Z2 row (`id | kind | anchor | rung | ...`), i.e., on the model-facing decision surface every turn. (3) The design never states the invariant "no Executive path branches on kind or label" — every described retrieval path happens to be type-free, but nothing forbids the compiler, pather, eviction ranker, or ACTION6 sampler from keying on kind, and the design elsewhere uses undefined class terms that flirt with exactly that: LEARN-ACTIONS gates on "the current context class," LEARN-RULES on "every referent class with an observed change," ACTION6 is "sampled over anchored referent classes," and Procedures carry a "context-class scope fingerprint." If "class" resolves to an LLM-inferred type, then achievement tests — the layer §9.3 already admits is the Goodhart surface — gate on inferred typing, and a turn-1 misclassification can strand a LEARN-* goal or exclude the small dull percept from ACTION6 sampling: the structure hiding exactly what it was built to surface. Stated intentions ("abstract; no game nouns") do not meet the mechanism bar the design applies to every other field.

**Design's response:** ARG does not inherit LINK-KG's ontology dependence because it refuses the design move that creates it: no LLM ever types a referent, and nothing retrieves by type. The only "ontology" is a four-value, game-agnostic kind derived by a closed machine mapping from Locator provenance (§2.2) — it makes no domain claims, so it cannot mis-fit an unseen game — and rule R7 forbids every Executive path (join, compilation, means analysis, eviction, frontier enumeration, ACTION6 sampling, view construction) from branching even on that machine-derived kind, let alone on the LLM's cosmetic labels: retrieval keys exclusively on Executive-issued ids, Locator geometry, signatures, grounding rungs, receipts, and goal bindings. Game-specific characterization is carried instead by receipted, revisable annotations — `controllable` and Rules/Relations earned from ConsequenceRecords, demotable with TTL, never deleted — so a referent "misclassified" on turn 1 is by construction reachable and re-typeable on turn 50: mislabels are cosmetic, DORMANT referents revive on any new evidence, and too-coarse categories are repaired by the FISSION lifecycle with per-receipt re-attribution. The claim is enforced, not intended: the Q11 R7 randomization test requires that randomizing every referent's kind and label leaves all rendered views, frontier enumerations, and compiled Commitments byte-identical, making type-driven unreachability a detectable harness failure rather than a possible silent behavior. The nearest genuine analogue of LINK-KG's misclassification risk in ARG is machine-computed signature/context-class confusion — a typing-free identity risk — which is separately admitted in §9.1 and metered by FSN and the post-fission false-TESTED delta.

### XR-6 — SURVIVES (round 3)
**Attack:** The attack decomposes ARG into published parts: LQCA already showed mechanical coreference joins improve LLM long-text comprehension, LINK-KG already ships a persistent canonical-referent cache with quantified gains (45.21%/32.22% reductions), so ARG = joins + cache + a goal list — systems integration, the default architecture-paper desk-reject. The vulnerable surface is real and the design concedes the lineage in its own words: §2.5 R2 says the no-naked-mentions rule is "the enforcement LINK-KG's prompt cache gestured at but confined to its coreference module," and the load-bearing §2.5 join is exactly an LQCA-class mechanical coreference device. If the paper's spine were the join alone, the reviewer wins. The design survives because it contains the three mechanisms the attack demands rather than stating intentions. (1) The unification claim is an experiment with a written kill condition, not a framing: P2 pre-registers that ONE switch (A1, ARG_JOIN=0) must degrade the grounding metrics (RGR, DTL) AND the goal-persistence metrics (GDS, ICR) "in the same runs," and is "Falsified if the metrics dissociate — then this is two useful patches, not one problem." (3) The delta from LINK-KG is schema-level: LINK-KG's alias cache grounds text in other text, while ARG's Referent is born from a machine-extracted anchor (LLM "never invents an anchor") and climbs rungs only through ConsequenceRecords whose match bit is Executive-computed against pre-registered predictions — a consequence-receipted grounding substrate static-text systems lack by construction — and goals are themselves referents whose `bindings` edges power both the join and the A5 retention arm, making the "goal list" a typed edge inside the same structure rather than an adjunct. The rebuttal holds — §7's collapse cells enumerate both the reference/position family and the late-horizon-adherence family, so selective gains there constitute dissociation and falsify P3 — but the paper should state that reading explicitly when writing up P3.

**Design's response:** ARG concedes the plumbing overlap outright — R2's mention substitution is the LQCA/LINK-KG move made inescapable, and §2.5 says so — and stakes the contribution on a prediction neither prior line makes or can test: that one aligned-referent structure simultaneously moves long-range reference metrics, position/salience-controlled metrics, and goal-persistence metrics, because goal drift's binding component is referent-grounding failure under salience bias. # ARG Benchmark Strategy — Recommendation

## Bottom line

**Do not switch the headline benchmark. Switch the experimental ORDER.** Keep ARC-AGI-3 as the paper's capstone claim (P0/P2/P4), but make a cheap static **mechanism track** — BABILong + LONGPIBENCH under the CRE position-controlled protocol (isolating J), and PlanBench-verification plus a multi-call adherence harness (isolating A) — the *primary development and gating benchmark*. ARC money is spent only after the static track passes pre-registered gates. This is the only ordering in which a dead thesis dies for hundreds of dollars instead of tens of thousands, while preserving everything only ARC provides.

**Why ARC cannot be demoted from the headline:**
1. **The grounding claim needs δ.** §2.3's scoped claim (causal-informational grounding relative to a transition function) is only defined in an interactive environment. On every static benchmark the claim self-deflates to "deterministic referential anchoring within a formal corpus" — the design says so itself. A paper whose *primary* result is the deflated claim is a coreference-preprocessing paper, and P2b's own falsified-if warns exactly this: if the contribution reduces to an alias cache, the thesis is dead.
2. **The parasitism defeater exists only on hidden Phase-B games** (no describing human corpus). BABILong/PlanBench items are exactly the pre-grounded human text Floridi's critique feeds on.
3. **The double dissociation (P2) requires both metric families live in one loop.** GDS-abandon needs a persistent agenda across calls with consequences; ConsequenceRecords, REOPENED, FISSION, the decoy arm — none exist statically.
4. **Owner's verbatim bar**: "test it on ARC AGI game complexity and demonstrate strong signal of progress in this field with ablation." Plus the Kaggle deployment story (offline small models) is the entire P4 narrative.
5. **Reviewer credibility**: after a rejection for unconditioned efficiency claims on one game, a resubmission that *retreated from the interactive benchmark entirely* reads as evasion. A resubmission that gates on it and reports the static mechanism evidence first reads as rigor.

Two structural gaps in the static candidates, stated honestly: (a) none exercises consequence grounding — the ENGAGED/CHARACTERIZED ladder idles, so the static track validates the *two repairs* (J, A), never the *unification*; (b) GDS-abandon needs a multi-call horizon, which PlanBench doesn't natively provide — it needs a thin execution harness (step-at-a-time plan execution with distractor turns, HIPIF-style) built on PlanBench domains.

## Staged plan

**Stage 0 — Static mechanism track (weeks, ~1% of ARC cost). The "primary benchmark" for development.**
- **Track J**: BABILong (QA1–5, 16k/32k/128k, position swept) + LONGPIBENCH (spacing swept), CRE-style collapse-cell reporting. Arms: {bare (pinned protocol), B-CACHE, J-only, FULL-static}. Pre-registered: FULL flattens position/spacing curves; B-CACHE closes canonicalization metrics but not answer accuracy at spread positions (P2b's static half); parity rows per §8.
- **Track A**: PlanBench verification (premise-closure vs VAL ground truth) + multi-call execution harness for GDS-abandon/GA/SCL. Arms: {bare, A-only, FULL-static}.
- This *is* §7's long-context track and P3/X2 — it moves from "transfer test at the end" to "gate at the start." Same code path, TextSpan Locators; zero throwaway work.
- **Gate G1 (progression to ARC):** J-track shows the pre-registered collapse-cell effect AND B-CACHE does not close it; A-track shows adherence effect. **Falsified-if:** J-only ≈ bare on middle-position items → the join thesis dies here, at static-benchmark prices, and the design goes back to review — no ARC spend.

**Stage 1 — ARC pilot (Sonnet feasibility, per owner staging decision).** FULL only, 3 public games × 3 seeds. **Gate G2:** completions > 0 on ≥2 games (P0 floor). If 0: publish nothing from ARC; the static track alone is not the paper the owner wants, so this gate is a stop-and-redesign, with the Q11/Q13 harness diagnostics (WER, ZCR, plumbing) consulted *before* blaming the architecture.

**Stage 2 — Full ARC protocol (the expensive block, only after G2).** The §8 factorial {J×A} + A3–A11 + decoy + backbone swap, ≥3 games × ≥3 seeds, parity rows, HRT. This is where P2's double dissociation is measured.

**Stage 3 — Small-model port + Kaggle.** Kaggle-eligible backbone on T4×2, WER per backbone, Z-tier sweep and R6 fallback tuning, Phase A validation → Phase B submission. Static track re-run on the small backbone (cheap) to give P4 a mechanism-level readout, not just a completions delta.

## Ranked alternatives

1. **Hybrid staging above (recommended).** Only option satisfying simultaneously: cheap falsification, the δ-dependent grounding claim, the parasitism defeater, the Kaggle story, and the owner's verbatim success criterion.
2. **BABILong+LONGPIBENCH as sole primary, ARC demoted to secondary.** The fallback if Stage-1 P0 fails irreparably. Publishable and credible, but the claim permanently deflates to referential anchoring, P2's dissociation is unmeasurable in full, and the Kaggle/P4 narrative is gone.
3. **PlanBench primary.** Tests only the A half; leaves the J half — backed by the single strongest evidence the project owns (the fact-xref 0/200→3/3 single variable) — unexercised. Wrong half to lead with.
4. **LongBench v2 primary.** Rejected: confounded, no mechanism isolation, gains unattributable — recreates the attribution weakness the reviewers punished. Keep as one appendix robustness row.
5. **BOOKCOREF/LegalCore primary.** Rejected: validates the prior-art layer (LINK-KG/B-CACHE) rather than the contribution. Use only as a small calibration study certifying that B-CACHE is a faithful LINK-KG equivalent (strengthens P2b), ~1 page of appendix.

One design amendment this implies: promote the §7 long-context track from "transfer test" to **pre-registered Gate G1**, and add the PlanBench multi-call execution harness as a named deliverable (it's the only missing piece for measuring GDS-abandon statically).
